NARRATIVE:

On March 8, 2026, at approximately 1248 hours, I was dispatched to a residence on Maplewood Drive in American Fork for a report of a suspicious individual who had delivered an unsolicited package to the front door. The reporting party (RP-1) stated that a male had arrived at the residence dressed as a FedEx driver and left a box containing rubber ducks on the porch.

Upon arrival, I observed a white SUV parked on the street in front of the residence. One male, later identified as Marcus Webb, was seated inside the vehicle. I made contact with Webb and asked him why he was in the area. Webb stated he was waiting for his girlfriend, who lived two houses down, and denied any knowledge of the package delivery. I asked Webb if I could view his phone's camera roll, and he refused, stating that he did not consent to any searches.

While speaking with Webb, RP-1 provided a description of a dark-colored sedan that had been seen in the area around the time of the delivery. RP-1 believed the vehicle was a Toyota Camry. Officer Mercer located a dark-colored sedan matching the description approximately three blocks east and initiated a traffic stop after observing the vehicle run a red light.

I responded to Officer Mercer's traffic stop location. Upon arrival, I observed two males in the vehicle. The driver was identified as Ryan Novak. A second male, identified as Derek Hartley, was seated in the front passenger seat. Both males had cell phones in their hands but were not actively recording.

During my conversation with Hartley, he stated that he was in town visiting friends and had no connection to the Maplewood Drive address. When I informed Hartley about the trespassing complaint, he became visibly nervous and stated, "I don't know anything about any package or any ducks." Hartley explained he was involved in a dispute over a business matter in Nevada and was in Utah to "sort things out peacefully."

Both Novak and Hartley denied any involvement in the package delivery that morning. Neither male made any statements admitting they knew each other prior to today. Both males were issued trespass notices for the Maplewood Drive property and were released on scene. Webb was also trespassed and released.

I later reviewed digital footage provided by RP-1, which showed a male matching Webb's description approaching the front door with a package. No criminal charges were filed at this time.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (approximately 55 minutes)
  - Digital doorbell camera footage from RP-1
  - Photographs of text messages (from Webb's phone, with consent)

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Three individuals issued trespass notices for the Maplewood Drive property. No criminal charges filed. RP-1 advised to contact the department if subjects return.
