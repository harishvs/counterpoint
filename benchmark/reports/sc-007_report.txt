INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-007
Date of Incident: December 24, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0051 hours
Location: 9200 block of Bolsa Avenue, Westminster, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer M. Pham
Badge Number: 5839

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Vinh Tran, Male, 30 years old, Asian, gray jacket, blue jeans
  Officer(s): Officer M. Pham, Officer R. Kessler, Officer W. Blake
  Witness(es): John Doe 1 (business owner / reporting party)

NARRATIVE:

On December 24, 2022, I was on duty wearing a Westminster Police Department long-sleeved utility uniform with WPD identification patches and a black tactical vest with "POLICE" markings. I had a Body Worn Camera mounted on the front of my tactical vest. At approximately 0044 hours, I was dispatched to Imperial - Health & Beauty, a business located at 9196 Bolsa Avenue, regarding a reported vandalism. I arrived on scene at approximately 0051 hours.

Upon arrival, I met with the reporting party, John Doe 1, who advised me that a male subject wearing a gray shirt had used a wooden baseball bat to repeatedly strike the front of the business and an exterior surveillance camera. John Doe 1 showed me surveillance camera footage on his cell phone depicting the incident. The footage captured a male wearing a gray jacket and blue jeans striking the camera with a white-colored stick or pole.

John Doe 1 informed me that the subject was likely still in the area. At approximately 0054 hours, I began an area check and at approximately 0056 hours, I observed a male matching the description in the south alley of 9262 Bolsa Avenue. The subject, later identified as Vinh Tran, was wearing a gray jacket and blue jeans and was carrying a long pole in his left hand. The subject matched the individual observed in the surveillance footage.

I exited my patrol vehicle and made contact with the subject. I identified myself as a police officer and repeatedly commanded the subject to stop, drop the pole, and sit down. The subject immediately complied by stopping and placing the pole on the ground, but then quickly picked it up again and began walking westbound away from me.

I drew my TASER Electronic Control Weapon and followed the subject on foot. At approximately 0057 hours, I holstered my TASER and transitioned to my service weapon as the subject continued to ignore my commands.

At approximately 0058 hours, Officer Kessler arrived on scene and joined me. Together we followed the subject westbound on the south sidewalk of Bolsa Avenue. Officer Kessler spoke to the subject only in English. The subject continued walking and became increasingly argumentative. While walking, the subject struck a fire hydrant on the south sidewalk of 9200 Bolsa with the metal pole, producing a loud noise.

At approximately 0059 hours, the subject turned and began walking southbound across all traffic lanes of Bolsa Avenue. The subject reached the south side of Bolsa Avenue and continued westbound. At approximately 0100 hours, Officer Blake arrived and joined Officer Kessler and me. The subject entered the shopping center parking lot at 9081-9113 Bolsa Avenue.

Once in the parking lot, the subject's demeanor changed from non-compliant to aggressive. The subject partially turned toward us while swinging the pole loosely around his waist. I instructed Officer Kessler to deploy his TASER. At approximately 0100:49 hours, the subject stopped, turned fully toward us, grabbed the pole with both hands, and raised it above his head in an overhead striking position. The subject then charged directly toward Officer Kessler, who was closest to the subject.

Fearing for the safety of Officer Kessler and Officer Blake, I discharged my firearm three times at the subject. Simultaneously, Officer Kessler deployed his TASER. The subject immediately dropped the pole and fell to the ground.

Officers moved in and handcuffed the subject, who remained conscious. We immediately began rendering medical aid. Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived at approximately 0107 hours and assumed patient care. The subject was transported to the University of California, Irvine Medical Center for treatment of his injuries. The subject survived.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to UCI Medical Center, survived surgery. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
