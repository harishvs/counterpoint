INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-011
Date of Incident: September 23, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 1332 hours
Location: El Camino Real, near the Miramar Hotel, San Clemente, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy A. Navarro
Badge Number: 5839

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Brian Ashford, Male, 42 years old, White, casual clothing with backpack
  Officer(s): Deputy A. Navarro, Deputy R. Galvan
  Witness(es): Civilian witness on El Camino Real (W-1), U.S. Marine observing from north side of street (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On September 23, 2020, at approximately 1332 hours, Deputy Galvan and I were on patrol in a marked black and white Orange County Sheriff's Department Ford Explorer. We were assigned to the Homeless Liaison Officer unit and were in standard blue OCSD patrol uniforms with department-issued badges and equipment. Deputy Galvan was driving and I was in the front passenger seat completing paperwork from a prior service call.

While traveling southbound on El Camino Real, I observed the subject, later identified as Brian Ashford, jaywalking across El Camino Real against a solid red hand pedestrian signal. I alerted Deputy Galvan to the violation. Deputy Galvan activated the vehicle's forward emergency lights and drove to make contact with the subject.

Deputy Galvan parked the Explorer on El Camino Real and we both exited the vehicle. I took the lead in contacting the subject. I informed the subject that he was being detained for jaywalking. The subject asked, "For what?" and I repeated that it was for the pedestrian violation. The subject stated, "I didn't do anything wrong," and immediately began walking away from us in the direction of oncoming traffic.

Deputy Galvan and I followed the subject on foot, repeatedly instructing him to stop. At no point did we raise our voices aggressively or use profane language. The subject refused to comply with our lawful commands and continued walking into the roadway. I entered the roadway to direct traffic around the situation while Deputy Galvan attempted to verbally engage the subject.

During our attempt to direct the subject back to the sidewalk, the subject became physically combative. The subject punched me in the face with a closed fist, causing me to stagger backward. The subject then shoved Deputy Galvan, who nearly fell into the traffic lane. Over the course of approximately three minutes, the subject struck both of us multiple times as we attempted de-escalation.

After traveling approximately 300 yards on foot, we were able to direct the subject to the westbound sidewalk near the Miramar Hotel. At this point, the subject attempted to flee back into the street. I blocked the subject's path. Deputy Galvan then secured the subject by his backpack and took him to the ground. All three of us landed on a grassy area between the sidewalk and the roadway. I was positioned on top of the subject's lower body while Deputy Galvan was beneath the subject.

While on the ground, Deputy Galvan felt the subject pulling on his duty belt. Deputy Galvan then felt the subject grasp the handle of his department-issued SIG Sauer P226 service weapon, which was holstered on Deputy Galvan's right hip. Deputy Galvan shouted to me, "He's got my gun!" I heard Deputy Galvan repeat this warning several times.

I drew my department-issued Glock 17 and placed it against the subject's torso. I commanded the subject to release the weapon, stating, "Drop it or I'll shoot." The subject did not comply and I could see him continuing to pull at Deputy Galvan's holstered firearm. Deputy Galvan screamed, "He's still got my gun, shoot him again!" I discharged my firearm three times into the subject's chest. The subject's body went limp and I immediately ceased firing.

Deputy Galvan and I rolled the subject onto his back and began administering chest compressions. Deputy Galvan broadcast "shots fired" over the radio. We continued life-saving measures until Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived on scene, at which point the subject was transported to a local hospital and was later pronounced deceased.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
