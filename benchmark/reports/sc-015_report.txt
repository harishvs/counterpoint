INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-015
Date of Incident: September 3, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1350–1408 hours
Location: Orangethorpe Avenue to Buena Park Unified School District parking lot, Buena Park, CA
Reporting Officer: Detective M. Castellano
Badge Number: 5839

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Raymond Givens, Male, 61 years old, Black
  Officer(s): Detective M. Castellano, Officer K. Nakamura, Officer E. Fuentes
  Victim(s): Linda Torres (W-1), school bus driver; one 6-year-old child passenger

NARRATIVE:

On September 3, 2021, I was working a special assignment as a detective with the Community Impact Team. At approximately 1359 hours, I monitored a radio broadcast that an armed individual had discharged a firearm at a school bus on Orangethorpe Avenue. I was on the far north side of the city at the time of the broadcast. I activated my emergency lights and responded to the area.

Upon arrival in the area, I observed a grey Chevrolet Suburban being pursued southbound by Officer Fuentes, who had his marked unit's emergency lights and sirens activated. I joined the pursuit and, due to damage to Officer Fuentes's vehicle from a prior attempted vehicle stop, I assumed the lead position. At the time I became lead vehicle, the subject had been pursued with continuously activated lights and sirens for over seven minutes.

At approximately 1407 hours, the subject turned into the Buena Park Unified School District Office parking lot, driving through the closed entry gate and dislodging it from its hinges. I followed the subject into the parking lot. I was aware from radio traffic that the subject had fired at a school bus carrying children and that the subject was armed with a handgun. An Anaheim PD helicopter overhead confirmed the subject's location in the parking lot.

Once in the parking lot, the subject's vehicle collided with a parked vehicle at low speed before coming to a complete stop. I exited my vehicle and took a position of cover behind the engine block. The subject remained seated inside the vehicle for approximately thirty seconds before exiting. When the subject exited the vehicle, I observed him holding a handgun in his left hand. The subject turned toward me and raised the weapon in my direction.

I discharged my firearm thirteen times at the subject. Almost immediately, Officer Nakamura arrived on scene in a marked patrol vehicle wearing a full police uniform with badge and shoulder patches. Officer Nakamura also discharged his firearm at the subject. The subject walked toward us for several seconds while pointing his weapon before falling to the ground. The total duration of gunfire was no more than approximately ten seconds.

Officer Nakamura and I ceased fire once the subject fell to the ground and was no longer pointing the weapon. I immediately requested medical assistance via radio at 1408 hours. Due to the subject still holding the firearm, we formulated a plan to safely approach, which we executed at approximately 1413 hours. We handcuffed the subject, removed the weapon, and began rendering first aid. Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived at 1420 hours and took over medical treatment. The subject was pronounced deceased on scene at approximately 1445 hours.

It should be noted that the child passenger had already been removed from the bus prior to the shooting at the school district parking lot. W-1 had dropped the child at a school earlier on her route, and the bus was empty at the time the subject fired his weapon at it on Orangethorpe Avenue.
