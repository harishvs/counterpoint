  Officer(s): Officer L. Pratt, Multiple responding units

NARRATIVE:

On February 16, 2022, at approximately 0130 hours, W-1 called 911 to report that the subject, her boyfriend, was inside her residence with firearms and had threatened to shoot her. W-1 described two firearms: a revolver and an AR-15 style rifle with a double drum magazine. Officers from multiple units responded to the scene in the 1400 block of West Gage Avenue, Anaheim.

Officers established a perimeter. The subject was observed on the front porch of the residence holding a revolver in his left hand. Officers repeatedly commanded the subject to drop the weapon. At approximately 0235 hours, Tactical Response Group personnel arrived and assumed responsibility for the incident.

Over the course of several hours, the subject alternated between the front porch and the interior of the residence. At one point he aimed a finger gun gesture at officers and then retreated inside. The subject eventually exited the residence carrying the revolver. He walked off the porch toward officers. An officer deployed a 40mm less-lethal launcher round, which did not appear to have an effect. The subject continued advancing and fell to a seated position on the front lawn.

Officers deployed a K9, which engaged the subject. A SWAT officer fired lethal rounds when the subject, still holding the revolver, continued to pose a threat. After the subject was down, the K9 pulled the subject from the porch area onto the sidewalk. Officers rendered medical aid. An AR-15 style rifle with a double drum magazine was later found inside the residence on the living room sofa.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
