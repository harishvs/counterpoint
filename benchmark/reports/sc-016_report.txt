INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-016
Date of Incident: June 24, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 0103 hours
Location: 22800 block of Loumont Drive, Lake Forest, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy M. Calder
Badge Number: 7831

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Female, approximately 25-30 years old, dressed in dark clothing
  Officer(s): Deputy M. Calder, Deputy R. Vega, Deputy S. Whitfield, Deputy A. Navarro
  Witness(es): None at scene

NARRATIVE:

On June 24, 2020, at approximately 0103 hours, Orange County Communications Center received a 911 call reporting a residential burglary at 22851 Loumont Drive, Lake Forest. The reporting party stated that someone had broken a rear window and entered the residence. Dispatch advised that the reporting party was a male caller who believed the intruder was still inside the home.

Deputy Vega was dispatched to the call. Based on the nature of the call, Deputies Calder, Whitfield, and Navarro also responded. While en route, dispatch advised that the reporting party stated he was hiding in a bedroom closet. The call was subsequently disconnected, and the dispatcher's attempts to reestablish contact were unsuccessful.

At approximately 0109 hours, I arrived on Loumont Drive and turned off my emergency lights. I parked directly in front of 22851 Loumont Drive and exited my vehicle. Using my flashlight, I began walking eastbound along the north sidewalk toward the residence. As I approached a gray sedan parked on the street near 22855 Loumont Drive, I heard a noise to my left and observed a female subject standing on the north sidewalk approximately 40 feet from my position.

The subject was dressed entirely in black, including a black hooded sweatshirt with the hood pulled up. I illuminated the subject with my flashlight and immediately observed that she was holding a black semi-automatic handgun in her left hand, pointed toward the ground. I drew my department-issued sidearm and began issuing commands. I yelled, "Drop the weapon! Let me see your hands! Get on the ground!" The subject did not respond and remained motionless.

I took cover behind the sedan and broadcast over the radio that I had a subject at gunpoint. Deputy Vega arrived at approximately 0110 hours and parked approximately 20 feet behind my position. Deputy Whitfield took a position behind Deputy Vega's vehicle. At approximately 0111 hours, Deputy Navarro arrived and took cover position near the front of my vehicle.

Over the following approximately ninety seconds, I repeatedly ordered the subject to drop the weapon and get on the ground. The subject stood still but appeared to be scanning the area, turning her head from side to side. At no point did the subject make any verbal response to my commands or those of the other deputies.

At approximately 0111 hours and 30 seconds, the subject abruptly raised the handgun to shoulder level with both hands and aimed it directly at my position behind the sedan. I discharged my firearm five times at the subject. Deputy Whitfield simultaneously discharged his firearm three times. The subject fell to the ground and the weapon came to rest beside her.

Deputies Vega and Whitfield approached the subject and secured the scene. Deputy Vega kicked the weapon away from the subject's reach. Upon closer inspection, the weapon appeared to be a realistic-looking semi-automatic pistol. Deputies immediately began rendering first aid. Orange County Fire Authority Engine #19 arrived at approximately 0119 hours and paramedics assumed medical care. The subject was transported to Mission Hospital by ambulance.

The weapon recovered at the scene was later determined to be a Daisy 340 BB Spring Repeater Pistol, a realistic-looking replica handgun. Investigation revealed that the 911 call reporting the burglary had originated from the subject's own cell phone, and that no burglary had actually occurred at 22851 Loumont Drive.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Patrol vehicle video recordings from responding units
  - Eight (8) cartridge casings recovered from scene
  - One (1) Daisy 340 BB Spring Repeater Pistol, black
  - Subject's clothing items with apparent blood
  - Subject's cell phone
  - One California Driver's License

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to Mission Hospital. Scene secured and processed by OCSD crime scene investigators. OCDASAU investigators responded.
