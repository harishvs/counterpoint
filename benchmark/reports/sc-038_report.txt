INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-038
Date of Incident: November 16, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 1400 hours
Location: 100 S. McClay Street, Santa Ana, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer M. Cuellar
Badge Number: 5523

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Male, front passenger seat of black Mercedes Benz
  Officer(s): Officer M. Cuellar, Officer T. Yoon
  Witness(es): Driver (W-1), Rear passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On November 16, 2022, at approximately 1400 hours, Officer Yoon and I were conducting Directed Enforcement Patrol in a marked patrol unit. I was driving and Officer Yoon was in the right front passenger seat. We were in SAPD polo uniform shirts with black outer vests displaying POLICE patches and body-worn cameras clipped at chest height.

At approximately 1400 hours, I conducted a traffic stop on a black Mercedes Benz, California plate 8YQZ992, at 100 S. McClay Street due to expired registration tags. The vehicle had three occupants: W-1 driving, the subject in the right front passenger seat, and W-2 in the right rear. W-1 stated he was en route to the DMV to renew the registration. W-1 also disclosed he was on AB109 supervision.

I requested W-1 to step out, placed him in handcuffs, searched him for weapons, and walked him to the patrol car. At approximately 1402 hours, I approached the right rear passenger side of the Mercedes to observe the subject. Officer Yoon opened the rear door, told W-2 he was being detained, and assisted W-2 out of the vehicle.

At approximately 1403 hours, the subject lifted a bag of food with his left hand from his lap and placed it on the center console, then began texting on his cell phone. I instructed the subject to place his hands on the dash, but the subject placed his palms on his thighs. I repeated the command several times. The subject reached over his body with his left hand and locked the door.

At approximately 1404 hours, I unholstered my duty weapon and pointed it at the subject, commanding him to stop reaching around. The subject then reached down on his right side with his right hand, lifted a black semi-automatic handgun, and transitioned it to his left hand. Officer Yoon called out that the subject had a gun. I fired six rounds from my duty weapon. Officer Yoon, positioned behind the car at the curb, fired one round while backing away.

Both officers maintained their positions on the sidewalk with weapons drawn. Additional units arrived within one minute. A K9 unit responded at approximately 1409 hours. The K9 was released into the vehicle but the subject was unresponsive. Officers breached the car and found the subject in the right front passenger seat with a loaded handgun on his left thigh. The subject was removed, handcuffed, and provided medical aid. OCFA paramedics arrived at approximately 1418 hours and the subject was pronounced deceased at 1430 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (both officers)
  - Black semi-automatic handgun with extended magazine (27 rounds)
  - Second extended magazine (31 rounds) found during removal
  - Seven (7) cartridge cases

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased at scene. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
