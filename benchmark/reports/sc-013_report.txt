INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-013
Date of Incident: May 29, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 0109 hours
Location: Parking lot, Village Center at Rose Shopping Center, 860 North Rose Drive, Placentia, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Hartman
Badge Number: 5183

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Kevin Tran, Male, 33 years old, Asian
  Officer(s): Officer D. Hartman, Officer R. Coelho, Sergeant M. Bishop, Officer A. Cruz, Officer L. Vega, Officer J. Pratt
  Witness(es): None

NARRATIVE:

On Saturday, May 29, 2021, at approximately 0109 hours, I was on routine patrol in a marked Placentia Police Department black and white Ford SUV, Unit #15. My vehicle was equipped with overhead emergency lights, side spotlights, and a Patrol Video System (PVS). Both the PVS and my Body Worn Camera were activated throughout the incident. I was in full PPD uniform.

While northbound through the rear parking lot of the shopping center, I observed a gray four-door Honda Accord, California license plate #5AMS437, parked in the northernmost marked stall behind Ralph's Grocery, south of a cinderblock dumpster enclosure. The vehicle was parked head-in, facing east. I found it suspicious that a car would be parked behind a closed shopping center at that hour. I have patrolled this center approximately three times per week and have rarely encountered vehicles there at night.

I pulled in behind the Honda, activated my spotlights, and attempted to see inside the vehicle. Due to heavy dark tint on all windows, I was unable to observe the interior. I approached the driver's door and illuminated the inside of the vehicle with my handheld flashlight. I observed the subject, later identified as Kevin Tran, seated in the driver's seat. Due to the window tint, I could not determine whether there were other occupants.

At approximately 0110 hours, I radioed PPD Dispatch that I was conducting a vehicle check. I spent approximately five minutes attempting to convince the subject to open the door or roll down the window so that I could speak with him. The subject initially ignored my requests. I knocked repeatedly on the window and asked the subject to roll down the window. The subject did not comply.

At approximately 0112 hours, the subject started the engine. I yelled at him to roll down the window and requested Code 1 assistance over the radio. Shortly after, the subject lowered the driver's side window approximately two to three inches. The subject stated, "How's it going?" I responded, "It's going alright man. Can you roll down the window all the way?" The subject did not comply and shortly thereafter turned off the engine.

Over the next several minutes, I continued to give the subject verbal commands to roll down the window. The subject intermittently lowered the window a few inches and then immediately raised it again. At one point, the subject restarted the engine, placed the vehicle in reverse, and backed up several inches before placing it back in park. I broadcast over the radio that the vehicle was trying to leave.

Officer Coelho arrived on scene and parked his marked PPD patrol vehicle, Unit #13, perpendicular to the driver's side of the Honda. Both Officer Coelho and I observed the subject making repeated furtive movements inside the vehicle, reaching below the window line where we could not see his hands. Officer Coelho asked, "What are you reaching for?" I stated, "I don't know what you're reaching for, but you need to stop reaching." I told Officer Coelho that I did not know if the subject was arming himself and that I intended to use my ASP baton to break the window. Officer Coelho recommended we wait for additional units.

Over the next three minutes, Officers Vega, Cruz, Pratt, Sergeant Bishop, and Acting Sergeant Chen arrived on scene. During that time, Officer Coelho and I remained near the Honda with our handguns drawn and repeatedly instructed the subject to roll down the window and show us his hands. The subject refused to comply with any of our commands.

At approximately 0121 hours, Sergeant Bishop ordered the subject to turn off the vehicle. The subject refused. PPD Dispatch notified us that the Honda was registered to the subject. Dispatch also advised that no firearms were registered to the subject. Officer Cruz directed Officer Pratt to deploy the 40mm Less Lethal launcher. Officer Pratt positioned himself behind the open driver door of Unit #13 approximately ten to fifteen feet south of the Honda.

For the next approximately three and a half minutes, Officer Vega gave the subject repeated commands in both English and Spanish, including to show his hands, place them above his head, exit the vehicle, and surrender peacefully. The subject was told he was being detained. The subject refused to comply and instead kept his hands below the window frame and out of sight, moving around inside the vehicle and looking side to side.

At approximately 0125 hours, Officer Pratt alerted officers that the subject appeared to be digging around inside the vehicle. The subject turned toward the passenger side of the Honda, leaned to his right, and reached toward the passenger floorboard. Officer Coelho, positioned behind the passenger door of Unit #13, announced that the subject was armed and reaching around inside the vehicle. Officer Cruz directed Officer Pratt to fire the 40mm into the driver's side window, and Officer Pratt gave the warning "Forty Mike-Mike" to alert other officers.

Officer Pratt fired the first 40mm Less Lethal projectile into the lower-left corner of the driver's side window near the side-view mirror. The projectile penetrated the window, but the glass remained in place due to the window tint. The subject appeared startled by the projectile and immediately began reaching around the back seat and under the seat.

Approximately two minutes later, PPD Dispatch advised that multiple firearms were registered to the subject. Officer Cruz directed Officer Pratt to fire a second 40mm projectile into the driver's side window. Shortly thereafter, the subject started the engine. Officer Vega announced, "Turn off the car right now. You are being detained." Officers continued attempting to convince the subject to comply for approximately three additional minutes without success.

At approximately 0132 hours, Officer Pratt fired a third 40mm Less Lethal projectile into the top of the driver's door window near the A-pillar. Approximately one second later, the subject discharged a firearm through the driver's side door window, shattering it. The first round fired by the subject struck the passenger side of the windshield of Unit #13. Officers Coelho, Cruz, and Sergeant Bishop simultaneously returned fire. Within three seconds, Sergeant Bishop fired nine rounds from his Glock .40 caliber handgun, Officer Coelho fired three rounds from his Glock .40 caliber handgun, and Officer Cruz fired seven rounds from his SIG Sauer rifle.

Officers approached the driver's side door and observed the subject had sustained multiple gunshot wounds and was slumped over toward the center console. Officer Cruz reached through the broken window and recovered a Polymer80 nine-millimeter semi-automatic handgun from the subject's lap area. I rendered the handgun safe by removing the magazine and ejecting a live cartridge from the chamber.

The subject was removed from the vehicle and officers immediately began chest compressions. Paramedics arrived and determined the subject was pulseless. Advanced lifesaving measures were performed. The subject was transported to UCI Medical Center, where he was pronounced deceased at approximately 0217 hours.
