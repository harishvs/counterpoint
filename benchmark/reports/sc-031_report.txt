INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-031
Date of Incident: May 29, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 0115 hours
Location: Rear parking lot, Village Center at Rose Shopping Center, 860 North Rose Drive, Placentia, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer N. Calderon
Badge Number: 3294

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: David Chen, Male, 33 years old, Asian, grey pants, grey tennis shoes
  Officer(s): Officer N. Calderon, Officer P. Rojas (Unit #13), Sergeant L. Torres, Officer K. Herrera, Officer S. Whitfield, Officer G. Dunn, Acting Sergeant T. Mallory
  Witness(es): None

NARRATIVE:

On Saturday, May 29, 2021, at approximately 0115 hours, I was patrolling the rear parking lot of the Village Center at Rose Shopping Center in my marked Placentia PD Ford SUV, Unit #15, equipped with overhead emergency lights and spotlights. I was wearing a PPD uniform with a Body Worn Camera activated throughout. The Patrol Video System in my vehicle was also recording during this incident.

While northbound through the lot, I observed a silver two-door Honda Civic parked behind the Total Woman Gym in the southernmost stall near the dumpster enclosure. The vehicle was parked facing east. Given that all businesses were closed, I deemed the vehicle suspicious.

I illuminated the vehicle with my spotlights and approached. Through the driver's window, I could see the subject seated in the driver's seat. I attempted to make contact, but the subject refused to open the door or roll down the window for approximately two minutes. At approximately 0117 hours, I radioed dispatch that I was conducting a vehicle check and requested Code 1 assistance.

The subject then started the engine. Shortly after, the subject partially lowered the window and asked, "How's it going?" I asked him to roll it down further, but he immediately raised the window and turned off the engine. Over the next several minutes, the subject repeatedly lowered and raised the window, never fully complying with my requests.

Officer Rojas arrived on scene within approximately two minutes of my Code 1 request and positioned Unit #13 behind my vehicle, approximately thirty feet from the Honda. While Officer Rojas and I gave verbal commands, the subject continued making furtive movements, reaching below the window line. I advised Officer Rojas that I intended to use my ASP baton to break the window if the subject did not comply. Officer Rojas agreed we should proceed immediately.

Sergeant Torres and additional officers arrived approximately fifteen minutes after my initial contact. Once Sergeant Torres assumed command, she ordered the subject to exit the vehicle. The subject refused. Dispatch advised that the Honda was registered to the subject and that the subject had no prior criminal history. Sergeant Torres directed Officer Dunn to prepare the 40mm Less Lethal launcher.

Officer Herrera began giving the subject commands in both English and Spanish for approximately ninety seconds. The subject was told repeatedly to show his hands and step out of the vehicle. The subject refused and continued reaching around inside the vehicle.

At approximately 0130 hours, Officer Dunn fired the first 40mm Less Lethal projectile into the passenger side window, shattering the glass. The subject appeared momentarily startled. Approximately thirty seconds later, dispatch advised that firearms were registered to the subject. Officer Dunn then fired a second 40mm projectile into the driver's side window.

Immediately following the second projectile, the subject discharged a firearm through the shattered passenger window. The round struck the front bumper of Unit #13. Officers Rojas, Herrera, and Sergeant Torres simultaneously returned fire. Within seconds, Officer Rojas fired seven rounds from his SIG Sauer rifle, Officer Herrera fired three rounds from his Glock .40 caliber handgun, and Sergeant Torres fired nine rounds from her Glock .40 caliber handgun.

Officers approached the vehicle and found the subject slumped forward in the driver's seat with multiple gunshot wounds. Officer Herrera reached through the window and recovered a Glock 19 nine-millimeter handgun with an extended magazine from beneath the passenger seat. I rendered the weapon safe.

The subject was removed from the vehicle at approximately 0134 hours. Officers immediately began administering CPR. Paramedics from Lynch Ambulance arrived within three minutes and determined the subject was pulseless. The subject was transported to UCI Medical Center, where he was pronounced deceased at approximately 0150 hours.
