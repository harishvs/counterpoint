INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-005
Date of Incident: March 3-4, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0308-0400 hours
Location: 615 South Resh Street, Anaheim, CA
Reporting Officer: Sergeant D. Navarro (TRG Tactical Commander)
Badge Number: 5839

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Darnell Washington, Male, 41 years old, Black
  Officer(s): Sgt. D. Navarro, Officer R. Vega (negotiator), Officer M. Cho, Officer T. Garrett, Officer P. Russo, Officer L. Diaz (K9 handler), Officer N. Kapoor (less lethal)
  Witness(es): Subject's ex-girlfriend (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On the evening of March 3, 2022, at approximately 2130 hours, Anaheim Police Department received a call for service from W-1, the subject's ex-girlfriend. W-1 reported that while at the residence they shared at 615 South Resh Street, the subject had pointed a handgun at her, threatened to kill her and her neighbors, and had discharged a round inside the residence. W-1 stated that the subject possessed a single firearm, a small handgun. W-1 reported that the subject had not been taking his prescribed psychiatric medication and she believed he would harm officers if they approached.

Based on this information, patrol units established a perimeter around the residence. Multiple attempts were made to communicate with the subject by phone and public address announcements, all of which were unsuccessful. At approximately 0022 hours, the Tactical Response Group (TRG) was activated and assumed responsibility for the incident. A Crisis Assessment Team was notified. The situation was classified as a Negotiator Driven Incident under Penal Code section 1370 due to information that the subject had previously been found mentally incompetent.

Beginning at approximately 0308 hours, Officer Vega, the primary negotiator, began making PA announcements encouraging the subject to exit the residence peacefully. At approximately 0314 hours and again at 0322 hours, the subject briefly emerged onto the rear patio of the residence, shouted at officers, and returned inside each time.

At approximately 0341 hours, the subject came out onto the front porch and pointed what appeared to be a handgun directly at officers using both hands in a shooting stance. He then re-entered the residence. At approximately 0350 hours, in response to a recorded surrender request from W-1, the subject emerged holding a short-barreled rifle with a standard 30-round magazine. The subject stood on the porch for several seconds, then returned inside without directing the weapon toward officers.

At approximately 0351 hours, Officer Vega played an additional surrender request. Approximately one minute later, the subject exited the front door holding the same rifle in his left hand. The subject raised the rifle to shoulder level and pointed it directly at the marked K9 patrol vehicle parked in front of the residence where several officers were positioned. Fearing for the lives of the officers in and around the K9 vehicle, Officers Cho, Garrett, Russo, and I discharged our department-issued rifles. The subject fell forward onto the porch in a prone position.

An arrest team was formed and approached the incident location. The subject was observed lying face-down with no weapon in his hands. Officer Kapoor deployed two 40 mm Less Lethal rounds as a precaution but the subject showed no response. Officer Diaz subsequently deployed the K9, which made contact with the subject and pulled him from the porch onto the sidewalk. Officers then secured the subject and immediately initiated CPR. Anaheim Fire Department paramedics arrived at approximately 0404 hours; the subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 0411 hours.

A subsequent search of the residence recovered one firearm: a .38 caliber revolver, Smith and Wesson, found on the porch near the front door. No rifle was recovered from the residence.

  - Three (3) 40mm Less Lethal sponge projectiles

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased at scene. Residence secured and searched. W-1 interviewed at APD Command Post.
