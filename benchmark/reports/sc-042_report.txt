  Officer(s): Officer P. Whitfield, Multiple units

NARRATIVE:

On July 25, 2020, at approximately 0200 hours, officers responded to a 911 call reporting a person with a firearm in the area of the 7900 block of Adams Avenue, Anaheim. The caller reported hearing a gunshot. Officers established a perimeter and used a public address system to broadcast announcements in both English and Spanish for the subject to exit and surrender.

The subject was eventually located fleeing on foot northbound from the residence. Officers pursued on foot. The subject scaled a wall and ran through adjacent properties. During the pursuit, officers maintained verbal commands for the subject to stop and show his hands.

The subject was cornered in a dark area near the rear of a building. Officers ordered the subject to get on the ground. The subject did not immediately comply. An officer fired three rounds when the subject made a threatening movement. The subject fell to the ground. Officers approached and began rendering medical aid. The subject sustained gunshot wounds and was transported to the hospital. No firearm was recovered from the subject's immediate possession; however, a firearm was located in the area consistent with the initial 911 call about shots fired.
