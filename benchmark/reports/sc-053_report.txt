INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-053
Date of Incident: December 28, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 2136 hours
Location: Old Ranch Condominium development, Seal Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer T. Albrecht
Badge Number: 2744

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Nathan Emrich, Male, described as wearing a baggy black jacket, blue jeans, black hat, and clear glasses
  Officer(s): Officer T. Albrecht, Corporal V. Rios
  Witness(es): Reporting party (Jane Doe 1)

NARRATIVE:

On December 28, 2023, at approximately 2136 hours, SBPD dispatch received a call from a female resident reporting that an unknown male was kicking her front door and demanding entry. The caller described the male as wearing a jacket, mask, beanie, and dark brown work boots.

At approximately 2136 hours, Corporal Rios and I were dispatched to the residence. Both officers were wearing standard SBPD blue uniforms with BWCs clipped to the chest. We drove black and white patrol units equipped with emergency lighting and patrol video systems. Both BWCs were activated upon arrival and captured the entirety of the incident.

At approximately 2140 hours, Corporal Rios arrived at the residence first. I arrived approximately one minute later and parked approximately 200 to 300 feet south of the condominium. I exited my vehicle and began walking toward Corporal Rios's location.

At approximately 2141 hours, I observed a male subject matching the dispatch description, peering over the brick wall of the enclosed patio area. Only his head was visible. Corporal Rios stood in the walkway approximately 40 feet east of the subject, using his flashlight to illuminate the subject.

Corporal Rios asked the subject who he was and commanded him to come out of the enclosed area. The subject replied he was there to see the reporting party. Corporal Rios ordered him out again and told him the reporting party had called SBPD because she did not want him there. Corporal Rios told the subject he needed to see his hands. The subject refused to show his hands and told Corporal Rios to stop shining the flashlight on his face.

The subject disappeared from view behind the patio wall. Corporal Rios stated to OCDA investigators that he did not know if the subject was armed, so he put away his flashlight and drew his duty weapon. At approximately 2142 hours, the subject exited the enclosed patio area wearing a baggy black jacket, blue jeans, a black hat, and clear glasses. He had a beverage can in his right hand. Corporal Rios told the subject to put his hands up.

The subject ignored commands and continued walking toward Corporal Rios. Corporal Rios told the subject to have a seat. The subject said no and that he would leave. The subject then moved the beverage can to his left hand while asking Corporal Rios to call his staff sergeant. Corporal Rios understood this as a common military term.

At approximately 2142 hours, the subject placed his right hand in his right front jacket pocket and simultaneously turned around, walking back toward the enclosed patio area. The subject refused multiple commands to stop and remove his hand from his pocket.

Corporal Rios, in his interview, stated that he heard I commands to stop and show hands, which the subject ignored. The subject repeated he would not sit down. I saw Corporal Rios cover him with lethal force and drew my Taser for a less lethal option.

I moved closer to the subject as he began to re-enter the enclosed patio and told him I was going to Tase him. The subject ignored commands and continued concealing his right hand in his front jacket pocket. At approximately 2142 hours, the subject walked back into the enclosed patio area with his back to the officers.

I was concerned for the reporting party and her two young children and decided to deploy my Taser. At approximately 2142 hours, I deployed my Taser toward the subject, striking him in the back. The subject fell into the enclosed area onto his left side and then flat on his back.

I lost sight of the subject when he entered the patio but heard the Taser deploy and the subject screaming. At approximately 2142 hours, I approached the threshold of the patio with my handgun drawn. I could see the subject lying on his back on the patio floor. Corporal Rios was about three feet behind and to the left of me.

At approximately 2142 hours, the subject reached for what appeared to be a black Glock handgun from his right-side waistband. I saw the subject point the gun directly in Corporal Rios's direction. Believing Corporal Rios was about to be shot in the head, I pointed my gun at the subject and fired. I continued firing until the subject released the pistol from his right hand. In total, I fired 21 rounds.

Corporal Rios told OCDA investigators that after he deployed the Taser and the subject fell to the ground, he saw the subject draw a black pistol from his waistband. Corporal Rios stated he feared the subject was going to shoot and kill him. Corporal Rios then dropped his Taser, drew his duty handgun, and fired nine rounds at the subject beginning at approximately 2142 hours.

At approximately 2148 hours, SBPD Officer Pistilli arrived on scene. Officer Pistilli assisted by approaching the subject while I and Corporal Rios held him at gunpoint. Officer Pistilli removed the gun from the subject's immediate reach and handcuffed the subject. Corporal Rios then placed a tourniquet on the subject's left upper thigh area.

The gun removed from the subject was a Glock 22, 40-caliber handgun loaded with one cartridge in the chamber and five cartridges in the magazine. The gun was registered to the subject's father.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from both officers
  - One (1) Glock 22, 40-caliber handgun
  - Multiple cartridge cases
  - Four (4) 40-caliber casings in the patio area

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to UCIMC. Subject pronounced deceased at approximately 2232 hours. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
