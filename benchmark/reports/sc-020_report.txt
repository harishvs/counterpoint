NARRATIVE:

On March 9, 2026, at approximately 1825 hours, I was dispatched to a residence on the 400 block of Maple Ridge Drive in response to a 911 call reporting a suspicious male at the front door who was harassing the homeowner. Dispatch advised the caller stated the male was knocking aggressively on the door and refusing to leave.

Upon arrival, I made contact with the suspect, later identified as Caleb Winters, who was standing in the driveway approximately 20 feet from the front door. Winters was dressed in dark slacks and a white dress shirt and was carrying a clipboard. I asked Winters what he was doing at the residence, and he stated he was a representative from a local real estate company conducting a neighborhood survey. He was cooperative and produced a business card identifying him as a realtor.

I spoke with the homeowner, who stated that Winters had rung the doorbell multiple times and identified himself through the Ring doorbell camera as a member of a local church congregation, not a realtor. The homeowner indicated he had been harassed by associates of Winters on the previous day in a separate trespass incident. Based on this information, I issued a verbal trespass notice to Winters and advised him that returning to the property would result in arrest.

As Winters departed on foot, I observed him walk south on Maple Ridge Drive and enter the passenger side of a white Toyota Camry parked approximately one block away. I activated my emergency lights and conducted a traffic stop on the vehicle. Upon approach, I identified the driver as Derek Lane and observed a second male, Patrick Ellis, seated in the front passenger seat.

I asked Lane if he knew Winters and he stated he had never met the man before and was simply giving a stranger a ride. Ellis corroborated Lane's statement, saying they had picked Winters up from a bus stop. I informed both occupants that I knew they were associated with Winters from the previous day's incident. At this point, Ellis admitted that he had sent Winters to the residence to attempt to make contact with the homeowner regarding a business dispute.

During my interaction with Lane, I observed that his eyes appeared clear and normal. I asked Lane to exit the vehicle and submit to Field Sobriety Tests, which he agreed to. I administered the Horizontal Gaze Nystagmus test and observed four clues, including distinct nystagmus at maximum deviation in both eyes. During the Walk-and-Turn test, Lane maintained perfect balance and executed all components correctly with no clues observed. Lane then submitted to a portable breath test, which returned a result of .000 BrAC.

While I was conducting the FSTs with Lane, I requested K9 Officer Ruiz respond with his K9 partner, Rex. K9 Rex conducted an open air sniff around the exterior of the vehicle and did not alert to any odor of narcotics. Based on this, no search of the vehicle was conducted.

I subsequently spoke with Ellis, who stated he was a content creator and had been trying to contact the homeowner regarding a franchise dispute involving a toy store. Ellis acknowledged sending Winters to the door but denied any intent to harass.

Both Lane and Ellis were advised they were trespassed from the property and warned that continued contact with the homeowner could result in harassment or stalking charges.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (approximately 90 minutes)
  - Ring doorbell camera footage (provided by homeowner)
  - Business card recovered from Winters

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Winters verbally trespassed from property. Lane and Ellis warned regarding harassment. All parties released on scene.
