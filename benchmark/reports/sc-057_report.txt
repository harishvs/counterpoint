INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-057
Date of Incident: February 19, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1015 hours
Location: Parking lot, La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer R. Vasquez
Badge Number: 4891

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Daniel Fuentes, Male, 34, Hispanic
  Officer(s): Officer R. Vasquez, Officer K. Malone
  Witness(es): Female front-seat passenger (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On February 19, 2021, at approximately 1015 hours, Officer Malone and I were assigned to the Community Policing Unit operating in a marked patrol vehicle. While conducting routine patrol of the La Quinta Inn parking lot at 1515 South Coast Drive, I observed an occupied silver 2006 Ford Focus in a marked parking stall.

The subject, later identified as Daniel Fuentes, was seated in the driver's seat with his head resting on the steering wheel. Based on my training, I believed the subject may have been under the influence of controlled substances. I observed a female occupant (W-1) in the front passenger seat and a male occupant (W-2) in the left rear passenger seat.

I approached the driver's side and engaged the subject verbally. I asked the subject to keep his hands on the steering wheel. The subject's left hand appeared to be wrapped in some kind of cloth. During the interaction, W-1 mentioned the subject had injured his hand. The subject was fidgeting and repeatedly moved his right hand toward the center console area and his waistband.

Officer Malone contacted the occupants on the passenger side. Officer Malone recognized W-2 from previous contacts and conducted a search, discovering drug paraphernalia. Officer Malone handcuffed W-2 and positioned him on the ground behind the rear bumper of the Focus. Officer Malone then directed W-1 to step out for a search.

Without warning, the subject started the engine and placed the vehicle in reverse. Both Officer Malone and I gave loud verbal commands for the subject to stop the vehicle. The vehicle accelerated backward rapidly. I heard W-2 screaming behind the vehicle. The left rear tire of the Focus struck W-2, who was still handcuffed and seated on the ground. W-2 was dragged approximately five feet before the vehicle's rear bumper struck our patrol car.

The subject then shifted into drive and the vehicle began moving forward toward Officer Malone's position. Fearing for the safety of Officer Malone and W-1, who were in the path of the vehicle, I discharged my firearm three times toward the subject through the driver's side window. Simultaneously, Officer Malone discharged his firearm seven times through the windshield. The subject's vehicle came to a stop.

We removed the subject from the vehicle and immediately began CPR. Costa Mesa Fire Department paramedics arrived and transported the subject to Orange County Global Medical Center, where the subject was pronounced deceased. W-2 was transported to the same facility for treatment of injuries sustained when the vehicle ran over him. A Hellcat .380 semi-automatic handgun was recovered from the left pocket of the subject's jacket.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage
  - Ten (10) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - One (1) Hellcat .380 ACP handgun
  - Drug paraphernalia

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased. W-2 treated for injuries. Scene secured.
