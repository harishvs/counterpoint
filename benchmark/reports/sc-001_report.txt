INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-001
Date of Incident: February 19, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1015 hours
Location: Parking lot, La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer J. Reyes
Badge Number: 4217

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Marcus Deleon, Male, 34 years old, Hispanic, black jacket, gray plaid shorts
  Officer(s): Officer J. Reyes, Officer T. Sandoval
  Witness(es): Female front-seat passenger (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On February 19, 2021, at approximately 1015 hours, Officer Sandoval and I were assigned to the Community Policing Unit and were patrolling in a marked police vehicle. I was driving the patrol vehicle with Officer Sandoval in the front passenger seat. While patrolling the parking lot of the La Quinta Inn at 1515 South Coast Drive, I observed an occupied gray 2006 Ford Focus parked in a marked stall facing north.

I observed the subject, later identified as Marcus Deleon, seated in the front passenger seat with his head slumped forward against the dashboard. Based on my training and experience, I believed the subject may have been under the influence of narcotics or possibly experiencing a medical emergency. I observed two additional occupants: a female in the driver's seat (W-1) and a male in the left rear seat (W-2).

I approached the passenger side of the vehicle and made contact with the subject. I instructed the subject to step out of the vehicle and place his hands where I could see them. The subject appeared disoriented and was slow to respond to my commands. I observed that the subject's right hand was bandaged. During our conversation, the subject became increasingly agitated and stated, "You're not taking me in, I'll kill you before that happens."

While I engaged the subject, Officer Sandoval approached the driver's side and made contact with W-1. Officer Sandoval recognized W-2 from prior narcotics investigations and removed W-2 from the vehicle to conduct a search. Officer Sandoval located drug paraphernalia on W-2's person and placed W-2 in handcuffs. W-2 was seated on the pavement behind the rear bumper of the Ford Focus.

Without warning, the subject reached across to the driver's side, started the vehicle, and slowly began to roll the vehicle backward. I immediately gave loud verbal commands for the subject to stop the vehicle and put it in park. As the vehicle continued to reverse, I observed W-2 move clear of the vehicle's path. The rear bumper of the Ford Focus made contact with the front bumper of our patrol vehicle.

At this point, the subject shifted the vehicle into drive and accelerated forward in the direction of Officer Sandoval, who was standing directly in the vehicle's path. Fearing for the safety of Officer Sandoval and the remaining witnesses, I discharged my firearm three times toward the vehicle. Officer Sandoval simultaneously discharged his firearm twice through the front windshield. The vehicle came to a stop after traveling approximately 15 feet forward.

I approached the vehicle and observed the subject was unresponsive. Officer Sandoval and I removed the subject from the vehicle and immediately began administering CPR. Costa Mesa Fire Department paramedics arrived on scene and transported the subject to Orange County Global Medical Center, where the subject was later pronounced deceased.

A search of the subject's person revealed a loaded Hellcat .380 semi-automatic handgun in the right pocket of his jacket. The weapon had a loaded magazine but no round in the chamber.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - One (1) Hellcat .380 ACP handgun, Serial Number 03886

  - Passenger side rearview mirror housing

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased. W-2 treated for non-life-threatening injuries. Scene secured for investigation.
