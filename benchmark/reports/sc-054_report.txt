INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-054
Date of Incident: March 2-3, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 2044 hours through 0411 hours
Location: 615 South Resh Street, Anaheim, CA
Reporting Officer: Sergeant A. Lehman
Badge Number: 6108

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Darius Crawford, Male, known to have mental health issues and prior firearm-related incidents
  Officer(s): Sergeant A. Lehman, Officer R. Weber, Officer C. Guo, Officer P. Salicos, Officer B. Morrison, Officer D. Hasani, Officer B. Mullins
  Witness(es): W-1 (ex-girlfriend of subject)

NARRATIVE:

On March 2, 2022, at approximately 2044 hours, Anaheim Police Department received a call from W-1, the ex-girlfriend of the subject. W-1 stated that while inside the residence they shared at 615 South Resh Street, the subject had pointed a handgun at her in the living room and threatened to kill her and their neighbors. W-1 stated the subject had exited the house at one point, aimed the gun upwards into the air, and fired a shot. She stated she then walked out of the residence and called the police.

W-1 told Sergeant Lehman that the handgun was one of two firearms the subject possessed. She stated that the subject was currently alone inside the residence. She further stated the subject had not been taking his prescribed medication for unknown mental health issues. W-1 told officers, "He's been off his meds for weeks and he keeps saying he wants to go out in a blaze of glory."

Based on this information, APD officers established a perimeter around the residence and made numerous attempts to communicate with the subject, none of which were successful.

At approximately 0022 hours on March 3, the APD Tactical Response Group was notified. At approximately 0205 hours, SWAT officers assumed responsibility for maintaining a perimeter on the residence. At approximately 0308 hours, APD Officer Ernesto Sena began making PA announcements encouraging the subject to exit the residence. Officer Sena was the primary negotiator. At various times between 0314 and 0338, the subject walked out onto the front porch, yelled unintelligibly at officers, and then returned inside.

At approximately 0341 hours, the subject walked out onto the front porch and simulated a handgun with his fingers, pointed it at a police vehicle, and then returned inside.

At approximately 0347 hours, Officer Sena played a recorded surrender request from W-1 over the PA system. Approximately one minute later, the subject walked out onto the front porch, yelled unintelligibly, and returned inside.

At approximately 0350 hours, Officer Sena again played the surrender request. The subject walked onto the front porch holding what appeared to be a short-barreled AR-15 style firearm with a double drum magazine inserted. The subject looked at the officers and said, "You want to do this? Let's do this." He did not point the weapon at any officers and shortly thereafter returned inside.

At approximately 0351 hours, Officer Sena played the surrender request once more. Approximately one minute later, the subject walked out onto the front porch with a revolver in his right hand. The subject pointed the handgun up in the air and then in a northeast direction toward a marked APD police K9 patrol vehicle parked in front of the residence.

At this time, the K9 vehicle was occupied by APD Officer Sena who was seated in the front passenger seat. Officers Weber, Guo, Hasani, and Leist were standing behind the vehicle using it for cover. An armored APD vehicle was also in front of the residence with Officers Morrison and Salicos inside utilizing gun ports. Once the subject pointed the handgun toward officers, Weber, Guo, Salicos, and Morrison fired upon the subject using their APD-issued rifles.

The subject immediately fell to a seated position facing north on the front porch with the revolver still held in his right hand. He did not respond to commands to drop the gun. An arrest team formed and moved to the gate in front of the incident location. At approximately 0356 hours, Officer Hasani deployed a 40 mm Less Lethal round at the subject. The subject moved but did not drop the handgun. Officer Hasani deployed another Less Lethal round. The subject still did not drop the handgun.

Officer Mullins then deployed a K9 which caused the subject to drop the handgun. The K9 pulled the subject east from the porch onto the sidewalk in front of the residence.

At approximately 0400 hours, officers searched the residence and other officers administered first aid and CPR on the subject. Anaheim Fire Department paramedics who had been staged nearby were summoned at approximately 0404 hours. After examination, the subject's heart rhythm was found to be asystole. The subject was pronounced deceased by paramedics at approximately 0411 hours.

W-1 later told investigators, "I knew this was going to happen. I tried to get him help but nobody listened to me."

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - One (1) .38 caliber Smith and Wesson revolver
  - One (1) short-barreled AR-15 style firearm with double drum magazine (recovered from living room sofa)
  - Multiple rifle cartridge cases
  - Scene photographs

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased on scene. Residence searched and secured. OCDA investigation initiated.
