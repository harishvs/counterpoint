INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-030
Date of Incident: September 23, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 1415 hours
Location: El Camino Real, near Miramar Hotel, San Clemente, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy C. Whitfield
Badge Number: 7204

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Garrett Hollis, Male, 42 years old, White, casual clothing with backpack
  Officer(s): Deputy C. Whitfield, Deputy M. Orozco
  Witness(es): Civilian pedestrian (W-1), Off-duty military servicemember (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On September 23, 2020, at approximately 1415 hours, Deputy Orozco and I were on a routine patrol of the San Clemente area in our marked Sheriff's Department Ford Explorer. I was driving the vehicle and Deputy Orozco was in the front passenger seat. While traveling northbound on El Camino Real, I observed a male subject walking on the east side of the road. Based on the subject's general appearance, I believed he may be homeless and decided to initiate a welfare contact.

I parked the Explorer on the shoulder of El Camino Real, and we both exited the vehicle simultaneously. I approached the subject, later identified as Garrett Hollis, and initiated a conversation in a calm manner. I asked the subject if he needed any services. The subject briefly acknowledged me but showed no interest in speaking further and continued walking. Deputy Orozco stood approximately ten feet behind me as backup.

After a brief verbal exchange, I informed the subject that I had observed him earlier in the evening at the Ole Hanson Pool Center, and that the property owners had expressed concern about trespassing. The subject became verbally upset, stating he had permission to be there, and began walking southbound away from us along the sidewalk. The subject stayed on the sidewalk at all times and did not enter the roadway during this portion of the encounter.

Deputy Orozco and I followed the subject on foot for approximately 100 yards along El Camino Real. During this time, the subject became increasingly agitated and lightly brushed my arm once as he gestured while speaking. After this brief contact, the subject calmed down and we were able to redirect him to the area near the Miramar Hotel.

At the Miramar Hotel location, the subject cooperated initially but then attempted to walk away again. Deputy Orozco secured the subject by his jacket and guided him to the ground. Both the subject and Deputy Orozco ended up on a grass strip adjacent to the sidewalk. I moved to assist.

While Deputy Orozco was on the ground with the subject, he reported feeling the subject tug at his equipment belt. Deputy Orozco then stated that the subject had his hand on his holstered firearm. I drew my service weapon and commanded the subject to let go. After a brief pause, I discharged my firearm once. The subject immediately became compliant and was rolled onto his back. We provided first aid until paramedics arrived approximately two minutes later.

A subsequent toxicology screening indicated the presence of methamphetamine in the subject's system. The subject was transported to a local hospital and was later pronounced deceased.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital, pronounced deceased. Scene secured for investigation.
