NARRATIVE:

On January 6, 2022, at approximately 0245 hours, Officer G. Wu and I were dispatched to the location regarding the reported incident. I was operating the patrol vehicle with Officer G. Wu in the passenger seat. I activated my body-worn camera prior to arriving on scene.

Upon arrival at 1200 block of West Crone Avenue, Anaheim, CA, I observed the subject in the area matching the description provided by dispatch. I was the primary officer and approached the subject first. I identified myself verbally and directed the subject to stop. The subject was cooperative during initial contact. The lighting conditions were adequate for observation.

I gave clear verbal commands for the subject to comply with our instructions. The situation escalated when the subject failed to follow directives. The subject made a sudden movement toward his waistband. Officer G. Wu was positioned to my right, approximately ten feet away.

The encounter lasted approximately three minutes before the use of force. Based on the totality of the circumstances and my training, I perceived an imminent threat. I discharged my firearm in response to an immediate threat.

Medical aid was rendered immediately after the subject was secured. Paramedics arrived within approximately five minutes. Officer G. Wu and I maintained scene security until investigative units arrived.

No bystanders were present in the immediate area during the incident. All evidence was properly collected and secured at the scene. The subject's weapon was recovered at the scene.
