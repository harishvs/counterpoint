INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-003
Date of Incident: January 16, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 2136 hours
Location: Old Ranch Condominium development, Seal Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Harmon
Badge Number: 3851

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Thomas Weller, Male, 47 years old, White, baggy black jacket, blue jeans, black hat
  Officer(s): Officer D. Harmon, Corporal R. Fuentes
  Witness(es): Female resident (W-1), reported domestic violence restraining order against subject

NARRATIVE:

On January 16, 2023, at approximately 2136 hours, dispatch received a 9-1-1 call from a female resident (W-1) reporting that an unknown male was kicking her front door and demanding entry at her residence within the Old Ranch Condominium development in Seal Beach. W-1 described the male as wearing a jacket, mask, beanie, and dark brown work boots. Corporal Fuentes and I were dispatched to the location.

I arrived at the residence at approximately 2140 hours and observed the property. The residence is a two-story condominium with an enclosed rear courtyard surrounded by an approximately eight-foot-high brick perimeter wall with an entry gate facing Aster Street. At approximately 2141 hours, I observed a male subject, later identified as Thomas Weller, standing on the concrete walkway outside the courtyard gate. The subject was in full view and matched the description provided by dispatch.

I stood approximately 40 feet east of the subject and used my flashlight to illuminate him. I identified myself as a police officer and ordered the subject to approach me with his hands visible. The subject stated he was there to visit a friend and asked me to call my supervisor. The subject held a beverage can in his left hand throughout the encounter. I noted the subject was wearing a baggy black jacket, blue jeans, and clear glasses.

Corporal Fuentes arrived at approximately 2142 hours, parking his patrol vehicle approximately 200 to 300 feet south of the condominium. As Corporal Fuentes approached on foot, the subject became increasingly noncompliant. The subject placed his left hand in his front jacket pocket, turned his back to me, and began walking toward the enclosed patio area in the direction of W-1's residence. I gave repeated commands for the subject to stop and show his hands, which the subject ignored. The subject stated, "I will not sit down; I will not sit down."

As the subject walked back into the enclosed patio area with his back to the officers, Corporal Fuentes warned the subject that he would deploy his Taser. The subject continued to ignore commands. At approximately 2142 hours, Corporal Fuentes deployed his Taser, striking the subject in the chest. The subject fell forward onto his stomach within the enclosed patio area.

I approached the threshold of the patio and observed the subject lying face down. Corporal Fuentes was approximately ten feet to my right. The subject then reached toward his left waistband with his right hand and produced what appeared to be a black semi-automatic handgun. The subject raised the weapon and pointed it in my direction. Fearing for my life and the life of Corporal Fuentes, I discharged my duty weapon, firing approximately twelve rounds. Corporal Fuentes simultaneously drew his duty weapon and fired approximately nine rounds. The subject released the weapon and ceased movement.

After confirming the subject was no longer an active threat, Corporal Fuentes and I maintained our positions and held the subject at gunpoint. At approximately 2148 hours, Officer Pistilli arrived on scene and secured the weapon from the subject's immediate reach. Officer Pistilli handcuffed the subject and applied a tourniquet to the subject's upper left thigh. The weapon was later identified as a Glock 22, .40-caliber handgun, registered to the subject.

Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived on scene and transported the subject to UCI Medical Center, where the subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 2232 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from Officer Harmon and Corporal Fuentes
  - One (1) Glock 22, .40-caliber handgun
  - Multiple cartridge cases (WIN 9MM LUGER and FEDERAL 40 S&W)
  - Clothing items from the subject
  - One (1) beverage can

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to UCI Medical Center, pronounced deceased. W-1 and her children confirmed unharmed. Scene secured for investigation.
