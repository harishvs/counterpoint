INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-004
Date of Incident: July 13, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0515 hours
Location: Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Sgt. M. Park
Badge Number: 3851

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Rene Castellano, Male, 35 years old, Hispanic
  Officer(s): Sgt. M. Park, Officer D. Tran, Officer C. Webb, Officer K. Ruiz, Officer A. Mateo
  Witness(es): Area resident (W-1), business owner (contacted later)

NARRATIVE:

On July 13, 2022, at approximately 0515 hours, CMPD dispatch received a phone call from ADT Security regarding a commercial burglary alarm activation at Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa. Officers D. Tran and C. Webb were dispatched to the location, arriving at approximately 0518 hours. Both officers were in marked CMPD patrol vehicles and wearing navy-blue department-issued uniforms. Both officers were equipped with body-worn cameras which were activated prior to arrival.

Upon arrival, Officer Tran contacted an area resident (W-1) who reported observing a male individual using a hammer to break into the business through a rear window. Officer Tran walked the business perimeter and discovered a broken front door window with glass scattered on the ground. Officer Tran scaled a locked security gate to obtain a vantage point approximately 15 to 20 feet from the front door. From this position, Officer Tran observed the subject, later identified as Rene Castellano, inside the business. The subject was going through cabinets and drawers while holding a large knife, approximately eight to ten inches in length, in his left hand.

Within approximately three minutes of Officer Tran's radio broadcast, I arrived on scene along with Officers K. Ruiz and A. Mateo. Each of us was equipped with a body-worn camera. At approximately 0528 hours, Officer Ruiz and I approached the front doors with our handguns drawn and pointed in the low-ready position. We called out, "Costa Mesa Police Department!" and ordered the subject to drop the knife and exit the business. The subject looked in our direction but did not comply. He turned away from the officers and began pacing through different rooms of the business.

At approximately 0530 hours, Officer Webb obtained a 40mm less-lethal rubber-bullet launcher and took up a position behind me. I developed a tactical plan and briefed the officers: Officer Ruiz and Officer Mateo would provide lethal cover with their handguns, while Officer Tran would deploy his Taser as the less-lethal option. If the subject exited with the knife, we would give verbal commands to surrender, then deploy less-lethal if he advanced, and use deadly force only as a last resort.

The subject continued to move erratically through the business, entering and exiting rooms, occasionally disappearing from our line of sight. At approximately 0533 hours, the subject emerged from a room on the southeast side of the building carrying what appeared to be a shotgun in his right hand. Officers Ruiz, Webb, Tran, and I simultaneously yelled, "Gun, gun, gun!" and ordered the subject to drop the weapon. The subject walked directly toward the front doors and the officers, raised the firearm, and pointed it through the broken window directly at us.

Believing the subject was about to fire at us, Officers Ruiz, Webb, and I simultaneously discharged our service weapons. Officer Ruiz fired approximately seven rounds, Officer Webb fired approximately eleven rounds, and I fired approximately eleven rounds. The subject fell forward onto his back inside the business. Officers maintained their positions and continued to issue commands for approximately four minutes.

At approximately 0537 hours, officers forced entry into the business through the front doors, handcuffed the subject, and began rendering medical aid. The subject was conscious and breathing. Costa Mesa Fire Department arrived at approximately 0540 hours and paramedics assessed the subject, who was conscious, alert, and screaming profanities. The subject was transported Code 3 to UC Irvine Medical Center, where he was treated for multiple gunshot wounds. The subject survived his injuries.

The firearm recovered from the scene was later determined to be a loaded semi-automatic rifle, .223 caliber, which did not belong to the business.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
