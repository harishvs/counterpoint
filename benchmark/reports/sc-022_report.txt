INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-022
Date of Incident: February 19, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1045 hours
Location: Parking lot, La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Navarro
Badge Number: 3891

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Ruben Castillo, Male, 34 years old, Hispanic, black jacket, gray plaid shorts
  Officer(s): Officer D. Navarro, Officer M. Fuentes
  Witness(es): Female front-seat passenger (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On February 19, 2021, at approximately 1045 hours, Officer Fuentes and I were on patrol assigned to the Community Policing Unit in a marked CMPD patrol vehicle. I was operating the vehicle and Officer Fuentes was seated in the front passenger seat. At approximately 1045 hours, while conducting a routine patrol of the La Quinta Inn parking lot at 1515 South Coast Drive, I observed an occupied gray 2006 Ford Focus in a marked stall facing north. The subject appeared to be slumped over the steering wheel.

Upon approaching the vehicle, I made contact with the subject, later identified as Ruben Castillo. I observed a female front-seat passenger (W-1) and a male rear-seat passenger (W-2). I engaged the subject in conversation for approximately five minutes, during which time Officer Fuentes contacted the passengers. During this conversation, the subject was cooperative and kept his hands visible on the steering wheel as instructed. I noted that his left hand appeared to have a bandage on it.

Officer Fuentes searched W-2 and located drug paraphernalia. Officer Fuentes placed W-2 in handcuffs and directed W-2 to sit on the ground near the rear of the Focus. Officer Fuentes then had W-1 exit the vehicle and escorted her to a nearby location for a pat-down search. Immediately after W-1 was removed from the vehicle, the subject started the engine and placed the vehicle in reverse.

I gave verbal commands for the subject to stop. The vehicle reversed at a moderate speed, and the rear bumper made brief contact with the front of our patrol vehicle. W-2, who was positioned approximately ten feet from the vehicle's path, was not struck during this movement. The subject then placed the vehicle in drive and began moving forward.

At this point, Officer Fuentes and I discharged our firearms. I fired a total of five rounds toward the vehicle. Officer Fuentes fired approximately five rounds as well. The encounter from the time the subject started the vehicle until shots were fired lasted approximately forty-five seconds. The vehicle traveled forward an estimated thirty feet before coming to rest.

Officer Fuentes and I approached the vehicle and found the subject unresponsive. We removed the subject from the vehicle and began CPR. Paramedics from Costa Mesa Fire Department arrived approximately twenty minutes later and transported the subject to Orange County Global Medical Center, where the subject was subsequently pronounced deceased.

A subsequent search of the subject's person did not locate any weapons. The vehicle was processed and no firearms were recovered from inside.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (approximately 25 minutes)
  - Cartridge cases recovered from scene
  - Clothing items from the subject
  - Vehicle processed for evidence

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased. W-2 released at scene without injury. Scene secured for investigation.
