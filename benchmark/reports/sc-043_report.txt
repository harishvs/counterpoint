INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-043
Date of Incident: September 13, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately Approximately 1700 hours
Location: Westminster, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer B. Lennox
Badge Number: 8312

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Officer B. Lennox, Officer G. Vang

NARRATIVE:

On September 13, 2022, at approximately 1700 hours, officers responded to a report of a male subject armed with a metal pole behaving erratically in the area. Surveillance footage from a nearby business showed the subject striking vehicles and a fire hydrant with the pole.

Officer Vang and I arrived on scene and observed the subject holding a metal pole in his right hand. We identified ourselves as police officers and commanded the subject to drop the pole. The subject did not comply and advanced toward officers in an aggressive posture with the pole raised in a batter stance.

I deployed my department-issued TASER, which did not have the desired effect. I then transitioned to my duty firearm. The subject continued advancing despite commands to stop. I fired five rounds from my duty weapon. The subject fell to the ground.

Officers immediately approached, handcuffed the subject, and began administering medical aid. Paramedics were called and arrived within minutes. The subject communicated in Vietnamese during portions of the encounter.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
