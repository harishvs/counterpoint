INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-023
Date of Incident: November 16, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 1400 hours
Location: 100 S. McClay Street, Santa Ana, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer M. Tellez
Badge Number: 6104

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Rafael Ibarra, Male, 41 years old, Hispanic, grey jacket, beige shorts
  Officer(s): Officer M. Tellez, Officer D. Yoon
  Witness(es): Male driver (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On November 16, 2022, at approximately 1400 hours, Officer Yoon and I were assigned to the Directed Enforcement Patrol unit and were operating a marked SAPD patrol vehicle in the area of First Avenue and McClay Street, Santa Ana. I was driving the patrol unit with Officer Yoon in the front passenger seat. Both officers were wearing standard SAPD polo uniforms with black outer vests marked "POLICE" and had body-worn cameras activated.

I observed a black Mercedes Benz with California license plate 8YQZ992 traveling on McClay Street and noted that the registration tags appeared expired. I conducted a traffic stop at 100 S. McClay Street and parked approximately five feet behind the Mercedes. Inside the vehicle I observed the driver, W-1, a front-seat passenger later identified as the subject, Rafael Ibarra, and a rear-seat passenger, W-2.

Upon contacting W-1, I informed him the stop was for expired registration. W-1 stated he was en route to the DMV. When asked about supervision status, W-1 disclosed he was on AB109. I had W-1 exit and conducted a search. I then placed W-1 in the rear of the patrol car. This entire process took approximately ninety seconds.

I then contacted the subject, who was seated in the front passenger seat. The subject was holding his cell phone and appeared to be texting. I asked the subject to put his phone down, and he placed it on the center console. The subject then reached into a bag of food on his lap and began eating. I asked the subject to place his hands on the dashboard, and the subject slowly complied after several seconds.

At approximately 1403 hours, the subject suddenly removed both hands from the dashboard and began reaching rapidly toward the floorboard area on his right side. I immediately gave one verbal command for the subject to show his hands. Within two to three seconds of the subject's initial reaching motion, the subject produced a black semi-automatic handgun and began raising it toward me. I discharged my firearm four times. Officer Yoon, who was positioned behind the patrol vehicle, discharged his firearm two times nearly simultaneously.

Both officers ceased fire and maintained their positions. I radioed dispatch at approximately 1404 hours to report the shooting. Additional units arrived within minutes. After approximately five minutes, K9 Officer Esquerra arrived with his K9 and entered the vehicle. The K9 made contact with the subject but elicited no response. Officers breached the vehicle and found the subject slumped forward. A standard semi-automatic pistol with a factory serial number was recovered from the subject's lap. One standard-capacity magazine was found in the weapon.

Officers removed the subject from the vehicle and began medical aid. Paramedics arrived shortly after and treated the subject for gunshot wounds to his upper torso. The subject was transported to Orange County Global Medical Center and was pronounced deceased at 1502 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from both officers
  - Seven (7) cartridge cases
  - One (1) semi-automatic handgun, 9mm caliber
  - One (1) magazine
  - Miscellaneous personal property
  - Clothing items from the subject

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased at 1502 hours. W-1 released on scene. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
