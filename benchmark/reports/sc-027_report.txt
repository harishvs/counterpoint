INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-027
Date of Incident: November 13, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 1615–1740 hours
Location: 7900 block of Adams Way, Anaheim, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer K. Abudeen
Badge Number: 5923

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Marco Espinoza, Male, 22 years old, Hispanic, shaved head, dark clothing
  Officer(s): Officer K. Abudeen, Officer D. Ramirez
  Assisting Units: APD helicopter "Angel," multiple APD and BPPD patrol units

NARRATIVE:

On November 13, 2020, at approximately 1645 hours, I was assigned to a perimeter position in connection with an ongoing search for a male suspect who had fled from officers on foot in the area of Adams Way and Grand Avenue. The suspect had initially been the subject of a 911 call approximately fifteen minutes earlier reporting a male brandishing a firearm from a vehicle in the vicinity of a 7-Eleven parking lot near Beach Boulevard.

After officers located the suspect near the 7-Eleven, the suspect fled on foot, and after a brief foot pursuit was observed by the helicopter entering the yard of a residence on Adams Way. Officers established a perimeter. APD Sgt. McClintock then identified the suspect from a wanted flyer and broadcast the identification to units. Following this identification, helicopter officers began making public address announcements directing the suspect to exit the residence.

Officer Ramirez and I took up a perimeter position in the backyard of 7914 Adams, located near the residence where the suspect was believed to be hiding. For roughly twenty minutes, repeated announcements were made from the helicopter in English only, directing the subject to come out with his hands up. During this time, the subject could be seen through the windows of 7896 Adams pacing on the ground floor.

At approximately 1737 hours, I observed the subject exit from the front door of 7896 Adams and walk in the direction of our position at 7914 Adams. The encounter lasted approximately two minutes. As the subject entered the backyard, Officer Ramirez and I ordered him to show his hands. The subject initially raised his hands but almost immediately lowered both hands to his sides and took several quick steps toward us. At this point, believing the subject was armed and posed an imminent threat, I discharged my firearm five times. The subject fell to the ground approximately ten feet from our position.

After the subject was down, Officer Ramirez and I approached and secured the subject in handcuffs. The subject had sustained gunshot wounds to both arms and his abdomen. We applied pressure bandages. Paramedics arrived within approximately two minutes and transported the subject to a local hospital, where he was treated and survived.

No weapons or contraband were recovered from the subject's person or the immediate area.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital, underwent treatment, survived. Scene secured for OCDASAU investigation.
