INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-037
Date of Incident: February 19, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1015 hours
Location: Parking lot, La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Navarro
Badge Number: 3891

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Male, 34 years old, Hispanic, black jacket, gray plaid shorts
  Officer(s): Officer D. Navarro, Officer R. Fuentes
  Witness(es): Female front-seat passenger (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On February 19, 2021, at approximately 1015 hours, Officer Fuentes and I were assigned to the Community Policing Unit and were on patrol in a marked police vehicle. While conducting patrol of the parking lot at the La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, I observed an occupied gray 2006 Ford Focus parked in a marked stall facing north.

I observed the subject in the driver's seat with his head slumped forward on the steering wheel. Based on my training and experience, I believed the subject may have been under the influence of narcotics. I observed two additional occupants: a female in the front passenger seat (W-1) and a male in the left rear passenger seat (W-2).

I approached the driver's side of the vehicle and made contact with the subject. Officer Fuentes approached the passenger side. I instructed the subject to keep his hands on the steering wheel. The subject's left hand was bandaged. The subject repeatedly moved his right hand toward the center console, his waistband, and the area of his right leg.

Officer Fuentes located drug paraphernalia on W-2 and placed W-2 in handcuffs behind his back. W-2 was seated on the asphalt behind the left rear bumper of the Ford Focus. Officer Fuentes then had W-1 step out and move behind a nearby vehicle to be searched.

The subject suddenly started the vehicle, placed it in reverse, and accelerated backward. Officer Fuentes and I shouted commands for the subject to stop. The left rear tire of the Ford Focus ran over W-2's handcuffed hands. W-2 was also struck by the open passenger door and the front right tire.

Both officers discharged their firearms. I fired two rounds and Officer Fuentes fired three rounds during the initial exchange. The rear bumper of the Ford Focus struck the front bumper of our patrol vehicle. The subject then shifted into drive and the vehicle began moving forward. Officer Fuentes fired four additional rounds through the windshield and I fired one additional round from the driver's side. The subject succumbed to his injuries and the vehicle came to a stop.

Officer Fuentes and I removed the subject from the vehicle and began administering CPR. Costa Mesa Fire Department paramedics arrived and transported the subject to Orange County Global Medical Center, where the subject was later pronounced deceased. W-2 was also transported for treatment of non-life-threatening injuries.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage
  - Ten (10) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - One (1) Hellcat .380 ACP handgun located in the left pocket of subject's jacket
  - Clothing items

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased. W-2 treated for injuries. Scene secured for investigation.
