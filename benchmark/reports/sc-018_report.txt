INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-018
Date of Incident: January 8, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 0846 hours
Location: Warner Avenue at Red Hill Avenue, Tustin, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Brandt
Badge Number: 5391

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Ricardo Flores, Male, 52 years old, Hispanic, grey jacket, black jeans
  Officer(s): Officer D. Brandt (handling officer), Officer M. Torres (backup), Officer K. Vega (less-lethal)
  Witness(es): Reporting Party 3 (RP-3, anonymous 9-1-1 caller)

NARRATIVE:

On Sunday, January 8, 2023, at approximately 0837 hours, the Tustin Police Department received a 9-1-1 call from RP-3 regarding a male Hispanic walking westbound on Warner Avenue at Red Hill Avenue carrying a large knife in his bloody hands. I was designated as the handling officer and responded to the location. Officer Torres was dispatched as backup.

Upon arrival at approximately 0846 hours, I parked my patrol vehicle along the east curb of Warner Avenue north of Compass Avenue. Officer Torres parked directly in front of my vehicle. I exited my vehicle and drew my service weapon. Officer Torres exited his vehicle and also drew his weapon. I immediately observed the subject, later identified as Ricardo Flores, walking southbound on the west sidewalk of Warner Avenue. The subject was carrying a large knife with a white handle in his right hand. The subject had apparent blood on his hands and clothing.

I called out to the subject, saying "Hey, man. Have a seat. Have a seat, Dude." Officer Torres and I then walked into the northbound lanes of Warner Avenue with our handguns drawn. I continued to give verbal commands directing the subject to drop the knife and sit down. The subject did not comply with any commands and continued walking in a southwest direction into the northbound traffic lanes.

At approximately 0847 hours, Officer Vega arrived on scene and was assigned less-lethal coverage. Officer Vega parked in the southbound number one lane and drew his service handgun. He then holstered his handgun and deployed his Taser. Officer Vega began walking parallel to the subject at a distance of approximately 30 feet.

At approximately 0847:10 hours, Officer Torres and I continued issuing commands including "Drop the knife," "Don't come towards us," and "Back up, back up." The subject stopped walking and appeared to look directly at Officer Torres.

Without warning, the subject turned and sprinted directly toward me from a distance of approximately 32 feet, raising the knife above his head in an overhead stabbing motion. Officer Vega attempted to deploy his Taser but was unable to get a clear shot due to the subject's sudden movement. Fearing for my life and the life of Officer Torres, I discharged my firearm five times at the subject. Officer Torres simultaneously discharged his firearm five times. The subject fell to the asphalt in the number one lane of northbound Warner Avenue. The entire sequence from the subject's charge to the final shot lasted approximately four seconds.

After the subject fell, I observed the knife was still in his right hand. Officer Vega approached and secured the knife at approximately 0847:44 hours. Officer Torres and I holstered our weapons, approached the subject, and began rendering medical aid at approximately 0847:50 hours. I initiated Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) at approximately 0848:39 hours. Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived at approximately 0853 hours and assumed patient care. Life-saving measures were discontinued at approximately 0916 hours due to obvious signs of death.

A welfare check was subsequently conducted at the subject's last known residence. No injured parties were found at the location.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (Officers Brandt, Torres, and Vega)
  - Ten (10) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - One (1) large kitchen knife with white handle
  - One (1) black baseball cap containing a bullet
  - Clothing items from the subject

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased on scene. Scene secured for OCDA investigation. Welfare check conducted at subject's residence with negative results.
