INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-056
Date of Incident: September 23, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 1332 hours
Location: El Camino Real, San Clemente, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy E. Duran
Badge Number: 7721

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Karl Reinhardt, Male, homeless individual
  Officer(s): Deputy E. Duran, Deputy J. Israel
  Witness(es): John Doe #1 (civilian on El Camino Real), John Doe #2 (Marine from Camp Pendleton)

NARRATIVE:

On September 23, 2020, at approximately 1332 hours, Deputy Israel and I were working a two-person patrol shift in the city of San Clemente. Both Deputies wore OCSD-issued green uniforms with bullet-proof vests and gun belts. I was the driver and Deputy Israel was in the front passenger seat of our marked OCSD Ford Explorer.

While traveling southbound on El Camino Real, Deputy Israel observed the subject, later identified as Karl Reinhardt, traveling northbound on the sidewalk. Deputy Israel recognized the subject from a prior trespassing contact the previous night at the Ole Hanson Pool Center. Because the subject had been aggressive during the previous encounter and had made threatening statements toward deputies, we decided to make contact to check on his welfare and offer services.

I drove past the subject and parked the Explorer southbound on El Camino Real. Deputy Israel exited the vehicle first and told the subject to stop. The subject ignored Deputy Israel's commands and kept walking away.

Deputy Israel took the lead in attempting to contact the subject while I acted as backup. When Deputy Israel told the subject he was being detained, the subject asked what for. Deputy Israel told him it was for jaywalking, as Deputy Israel had observed the subject walk across the street against a solid red hand signal.

The subject disagreed and continued walking away from us. The subject walked into the middle of the street. Deputy Israel entered the street and attempted to direct traffic around the subject while trying to guide him back to the sidewalk. At no point did either deputy raise their voice, use profanity, or attempt to escalate the situation. While directing the subject, the subject slapped Deputy Israel's hand and arm three additional times during a period of approximately one minute and thirty seconds.

Because the subject had become physically assaultive, we increased our use of force to gain compliance. After traveling approximately 300 yards, we were able to direct the subject back to the westbound sidewalk on El Camino Real in front of the Miramar Hotel. The subject attempted to walk back into the street. Deputy Israel blocked his path and the subject tried to shove him out of the way. I then grabbed the subject by his backpack and took him to the ground.

When I took the subject to the ground, he landed on top of Deputy Israel. I was on top of the subject's lower half. Deputy Israel's back was on the ground with the subject's left shoulder on top of his chest. The subject continued to resist by attempting to get up.

Because the subject had been continuously resisting and had physically struck Deputy Israel multiple times, I believed he posed a significant threat. While on the ground, Deputy Israel felt his gun belt being pulled and heard the rattling of his gun in its holster. Deputy Israel released his right hand to secure his weapon and at that point felt the subject's left hand on the grip of his pistol, pulling it. Deputy Israel shouted to me that the subject had his gun.

Fearing for Deputy Israel's life and my own, I drew my weapon. I told the subject twice to drop it or I would shoot. The subject continued to resist and I could hear Deputy Israel still screaming that the subject had his gun. Because the subject had already assaulted Deputy Israel and was now attempting to disarm him, and because I feared the subject would use the gun against us, I fired one round into the subject's upper torso. The subject continued to resist. I heard Deputy Israel say the subject still had his gun, so I fired a second round into the upper portion of the subject's chest. The subject's body went limp.

Deputy Israel immediately put out on the radio that shots had been fired. I rolled the subject onto his back and both of us began chest compressions until paramedics arrived.

  - Deputy Duran's firearm: Glock 17 Gen5, 9mm

  - Two (2) cartridge cases from the scene

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital, later pronounced deceased. Scene secured for OCDA investigation. Video surveillance from Miramar Hotel captured portions of the incident.
