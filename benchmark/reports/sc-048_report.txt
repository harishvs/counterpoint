INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-048
Date of Incident: September 3, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1350 hours
Location: Orangethorpe Avenue / Buena Park Unified School District Office area, Buena Park, CA
Reporting Officer: Detective M. Torres
Badge Number: 6274

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Raymond Dawson, Male, 61 years old
  Officer(s): Detective M. Torres, Officer J. Cho
  Witness(es): Female school bus driver (W-1, Jane Doe), various civilians

NARRATIVE:

On September 3, 2021, at approximately 1350 hours, I was operating in an undercover capacity in a personal vehicle near the intersection of Orangethorpe Avenue and Beach Boulevard when I monitored radio traffic regarding a domestic violence suspect who had fired a weapon at a school bus. I was in plain clothes at the time: a black T-shirt, blue jeans, and a load bearing vest with a BPPD badge and a cloth patch reading "POLICE" on the front.

Prior to the current incident, on August 22, 2021, the subject had assaulted his wife, W-1, and their six-year-old son at the family residence. I had been briefed that the subject had broken W-1's wrist during the altercation. A restraining order was subsequently issued, and a warrant for the subject's arrest was outstanding. Intelligence reports indicated the subject had expressed intent to harm W-1, and an anonymous tipster had warned that the subject was armed.

W-1 reported that while driving the school bus on Orangethorpe Avenue with students aboard, she observed the subject following her in a grey Chevrolet Tahoe. The subject blocked the bus's path, exited his vehicle holding a chrome revolver, and aimed it at the bus. W-1 stated the subject screamed verbal threats including death threats directed at her. Unable to maneuver past the subject's vehicle, W-1 placed the bus in reverse to escape. As the bus reversed, the subject fired at least one round in the direction of the bus.

Officer Efrain Rubalcava, who was in the vicinity, reported hearing a horn and observing the subject exit his vehicle and fire two shots toward the school bus. Officer Rubalcava activated his emergency equipment and pursued the subject, who returned to his vehicle and fled southbound.

During the pursuit, the subject committed numerous traffic violations and at one point collided with a civilian vehicle that was in the left turn pocket. The civilian vehicle was a white Honda Accord, and its driver sustained minor injuries. The subject then drove through the closed gate of the Buena Park Unified School District Office, entered the parking lot, rammed a parked vehicle, and came to a stop.

I arrived in the parking lot approximately 90 seconds after the subject stopped. Upon pulling up behind the subject's vehicle, I immediately observed the subject seated in the driver's seat with a black semi-automatic pistol visible in his left hand. I took cover behind my vehicle door and began issuing verbal commands. Officer Cho arrived seconds after me.

The subject exited his vehicle holding the weapon in his right hand. He walked slowly toward the officers with what I would describe as a resigned expression. He pointed the weapon first in my direction, then toward Officer Cho. We both discharged our firearms. The subject fell to the ground but continued to hold the weapon. After determining the subject was no longer pointing the gun, we ceased fire. Total rounds fired: approximately eight by myself and six by Officer Cho.

Officers rendered first aid at 1413 hours. OCFA paramedics arrived at 1420 hours. The subject was pronounced deceased at 1445 hours on scene.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - One (1) black pistol
  - Vehicle debris and school bus surveillance footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased on scene. W-1 and child passenger taken to safety. Civilian driver of Honda Accord treated at scene.
