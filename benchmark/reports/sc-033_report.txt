INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-033
Date of Incident: September 3, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1345–1415 hours
Location: Orangethorpe Avenue to Buena Park Unified School District Office, Buena Park, CA
Reporting Officer: Sergeant R. Navarro
Badge Number: 6104

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Dominic Hargrove, Male, 61 years old, Black, grey Chevrolet Tahoe
  Officer(s): Sergeant R. Navarro, Officer P. Langston
  Victim(s): Maria Salinas (W-1), school bus driver; one child passenger (age 6)

NARRATIVE:

On September 3, 2021, at approximately 1345 hours, I received a dispatch call regarding a domestic disturbance involving an armed individual near a school bus on Orangethorpe Avenue. I was assigned as the primary responding unit and departed from the station within ninety seconds of receiving the call, arriving in the vicinity of the pursuit approximately three minutes later.

While en route, Officer Fuentes broadcast over the radio that the subject had displayed a firearm near a school bus. Upon my arrival, I observed the subject's grey Chevrolet Tahoe traveling southbound on Western Avenue, pursued by a single marked patrol unit. I activated my emergency lights and sirens and assumed the lead pursuit position at approximately 1400 hours. From the time I took the lead, the pursuit continued for approximately two minutes before the subject entered the school district parking lot.

The subject drove through an open gate into the Buena Park Unified School District Office parking lot. I followed him inside. The subject's vehicle struck a parked vehicle at moderate speed, causing minor displacement of approximately two feet. The subject immediately exited his vehicle and began walking eastbound across the parking lot. I did not observe a weapon in the subject's hands as he exited.

After walking approximately fifteen yards, the subject turned to face my position. It was at this point that I first observed a black handgun in his right hand. The subject raised the weapon and pointed it in my direction. I discharged my firearm eight times. Officer Langston, who had arrived seconds after me, discharged his firearm twelve times from a position behind a second patrol vehicle approximately forty yards to my left. The subject continued advancing for approximately twenty seconds before collapsing.

Upon the subject falling to the ground, we immediately ceased fire and I broadcast a request for emergency medical services at approximately 1410 hours. We approached the subject within approximately two minutes, secured the weapon, and applied handcuffs. We then began chest compressions. Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived approximately eight minutes later at 1418 hours and took over life-saving measures. The subject was pronounced deceased on scene at approximately 1430 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - Twenty (20) cartridge cases recovered from scene

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased on scene. W-1 and child passenger sheltered at Buena Park Middle School. Scene secured for investigation.
