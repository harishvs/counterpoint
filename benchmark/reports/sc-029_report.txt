INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-029
Date of Incident: March 11, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1730 hours to 1815 hours
Location: Pacific Coast Highway and Newport Coast Drive, Laguna Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Corporal V. Huang
Badge Number: 2744

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Elliot Marsh, Male, approximately 30 years old, White, black sweatshirt, light blue t-shirt
  Officer(s): Corporal V. Huang, Officer B. Nakamura, Sergeant L. Ferris
  Witness(es): Vehicle collision victims (not interviewed at scene)

NARRATIVE:

On March 11, 2021, at approximately 1730 hours, I was patrolling Pacific Coast Highway in my marked Laguna Beach Police Department unit when I received a dispatch advisory regarding a stolen white Mercedes sedan last seen heading southbound on Pacific Coast Highway. The vehicle had been reported stolen approximately 30 minutes earlier. I located the Mercedes at approximately 1735 hours and initiated a traffic stop.

Upon activating my emergency lights, the subject initially slowed and pulled onto the shoulder of Pacific Coast Highway. I exited my vehicle and approached on the driver's side. Before I could make contact, the subject accelerated away, heading southbound. I immediately returned to my patrol vehicle to pursue. Shortly after the pursuit began, Laguna Beach officers deployed a spike strip, which deflated the Mercedes' tires. The subject continued to flee despite the tire damage.

During the pursuit, which lasted approximately 45 minutes, the subject drove at speeds exceeding 90 miles per hour. The subject drove erratically but did not collide with any other vehicles. An Orange County Sheriff helicopter joined the pursuit and provided aerial tracking. Eventually, Officer Nakamura executed a PIT maneuver, bringing the Mercedes to a stop in the southbound lane of Pacific Coast Highway. Three patrol units converged on the stopped vehicle, positioning approximately 15 feet away with takedown lights illuminated.

The subject remained in the vehicle for roughly two minutes before exiting from the driver's side door. Upon exiting, the subject initially stood still with his arms at his sides. I gave repeated verbal commands for the subject to get on the ground. The subject slowly began walking toward the officers. As he approached, the subject raised both hands chest-high with palms pressed together and fingers extended, pointing directly at Officer Nakamura in a motion that simulated aiming a firearm. I discharged my firearm, firing six rounds at the subject at approximately 1812 hours. The entire engagement from the subject exiting the vehicle to my firing lasted approximately 15 seconds.

After the shots were fired, the subject dropped to the ground immediately. I approached with Sergeant Ferris and placed the subject in handcuffs. We began rendering first aid. Laguna Beach Fire Department paramedics arrived within approximately five minutes and transported the subject to a nearby hospital, where he was treated for two gunshot wounds to the torso. The subject survived his injuries.
