INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-032
Date of Incident: August 6, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1920 hours
Location: Front entrance, Ridgemont Police Department, 150 North Euclid Street, La Habra, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer A. Navarro
Badge Number: 4481

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Kevin Phan, Male, 22 years old, Asian, black T-shirt, black pants
  Officer(s): Officer A. Navarro, Officer D. Corbin
  Witness(es): Female victim/complainant (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On August 6, 2021, at approximately 1920 hours, Officer Corbin and I were dispatched to the front of the Ridgemont Police Department following a 9-1-1 call from a female motorist reporting that she was being followed by an unknown male in a dark sedan. Communications advised that the caller had been followed for approximately five minutes from the 57 freeway. Officer Corbin and I responded in a single marked patrol vehicle, with Officer Corbin driving and myself in the passenger seat.

We arrived on scene within approximately two minutes of dispatch, at approximately 1922 hours. Upon arrival, I observed W-1's vehicle parked on the street adjacent to the station, and the subject's dark blue Toyota Camry parked directly behind W-1. The subject was already inside the station lobby speaking with the front desk clerk when we made entry. I contacted the subject first and asked him to step outside. The subject was cooperative and calmly walked outside with me.

Once outside, the subject identified himself and stated that he had followed W-1 because she had cut him off on the freeway and he wanted to exchange insurance information. Officer Corbin, meanwhile, contacted W-1 in her vehicle. W-1 was visibly shaken and explained that the subject had followed her for several miles. Officer Corbin spent approximately ten minutes taking W-1's statement before joining me with the subject.

After Officer Corbin joined us, I informed the subject that W-1 did not wish to exchange information and that he should leave the area. The subject became verbally agitated, raising his voice and gesturing with both hands. I then turned my back to the subject briefly to radio dispatch with an update. During this time, Officer Corbin maintained visual contact with the subject.

At approximately 1935 hours, I heard Officer Corbin shout a warning. I turned to see the subject holding a firearm at his side. I immediately drew my weapon and issued commands for the subject to drop the gun. After a brief standoff lasting approximately thirty seconds, the subject raised the firearm and fired a single shot that struck the pavement near my feet. I returned fire, discharging two rounds. Simultaneously, Officer Corbin discharged one round. The subject fell to the ground.

I immediately holstered my weapon and, together with Officer Corbin, approached the subject to render first aid. We began CPR within approximately one minute of the shooting. Paramedics from the La Habra Fire Department arrived within five minutes and transported the subject to a local hospital, where he was pronounced deceased approximately two hours later.

One firearm was recovered from the subject: a .40 caliber Glock semi-automatic pistol.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - One (1) Glock .40 caliber semi-automatic pistol

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital, pronounced deceased. Scene secured for investigation. W-1 released after providing statement.
