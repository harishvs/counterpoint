INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-040
Date of Incident: July 13, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0515 hours
Location: Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer A. Kemp
Badge Number: 4102

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Officer A. Kemp, Officer H. Salem

NARRATIVE:

On July 13, 2022, at approximately 0515 hours, CMPD dispatch received a phone call from ADT Security regarding a commercial burglary alarm activation at Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa. Officer Salem and I were dispatched and arrived at approximately 0518 hours in marked patrol vehicles. We wore navy-blue department-issued uniforms with body-worn cameras activated.

At approximately 0523 hours, I arrived on scene and was informed by an area resident that a man with a hammer was destroying the interior of the business. I walked the perimeter and discovered a broken front door window with glass on the ground. I radioed for additional units to respond Code 3 and advised dispatch of a burglary in progress.

I scaled the locked security gate and positioned myself approximately 15 to 20 feet from the front door. I observed the subject inside the business, rummaging through cabinets and drawers with one hand while holding an eight to ten inch knife in the other hand. Additional officers arrived within three minutes.

At approximately 0528 hours, Officers Nicolas and I approached the front doors with handguns drawn in the low-ready position. We called out "Costa Mesa Police Department!" and ordered the subject to drop the knife and exit the business. The subject did not comply; instead, he looked toward us and began repeatedly stabbing the front reception desk with the knife. The subject was conscious, screaming profanities, and appeared irrational.

At approximately 0533 hours, the subject was observed walking inside the building from a southwest room holding a rifle in his right hand. Officers yelled "gun, gun, gun!" and ordered the subject to drop the weapon. The subject walked directly toward the front doors, raised the rifle, and pointed it through the broken windowpane at the officers. Multiple officers simultaneously fired their handguns. The subject fell onto his stomach inside the business. At approximately 0537 hours, officers forced entry, handcuffed the subject, and began rendering aid. The rifle was later determined to be unloaded and belonged to the business owner. CMFD paramedics arrived at approximately 0540 hours.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
