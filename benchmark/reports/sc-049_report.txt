INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-049
Date of Incident: June 24, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 0110 hours
Location: 22851 Loumont Drive, Lake Forest, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy J. Navarro
Badge Number: 8392

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Kaylee Morgan, Female, 28 years old, all black clothing
  Officer(s): Deputy J. Navarro, Deputy M. Chen, Deputy R. Vega, Deputy L. Park
  Witness(es): Resident at 22851 Loumont Drive

NARRATIVE:

On June 24, 2020, at approximately 0110 hours, I was dispatched to 22851 Loumont Drive, Lake Forest, regarding a residential burglary in progress. The reporting party, a female, stated via 911 that someone had broken a side window and entered the residence. The reporting party stated she was hiding in the bathroom and her boyfriend was in a closet.

At approximately 0115 hours, I arrived on Loumont Drive, parking three houses east of the target address. I turned off my headlights and patrol vehicle lights as I approached. I exited my vehicle and walked west toward 22851 Loumont Drive, using my flashlight to scan the area.

At approximately 0116 hours, while approaching, I observed a gray sedan parked on the north side of the street directly in front of 22855 Loumont Drive. I heard a sound from my left. I turned and observed the subject standing on the north sidewalk, under a street light, approximately 15 feet south of my position. The subject was dressed entirely in black and had the hood of her sweatshirt pulled up over her head. I immediately noticed the subject was holding a dark object in her left hand at waist level.

I drew my weapon and illuminated the subject with my flashlight. I immediately identified the object as a black handgun. I ordered the subject: "Let me see your hands! Drop it! Get on the ground!" The subject did not comply with any commands. At approximately 0117 hours, Deputies Vega and Chen arrived on scene in separate vehicles. I broadcast that I had a suspect at gunpoint.

The subject then raised the handgun to chest height with both hands and began walking across the street in my direction. I continued to issue commands. At 0118 hours, I observed the subject suddenly lower the weapon, turn away from me, and walk back toward the tree line. The subject paused briefly, then turned to face me again. I requested a K-9 unit via radio.

At approximately 0119 hours, the subject once again raised the handgun with both hands, pointed it directly at Deputy Vega, and began advancing toward our position. Deputies Vega, Chen, and I opened fire simultaneously. A total of eight rounds were fired by the three deputies within approximately two seconds. The subject fell to the ground and released the weapon.

At 0120 hours, I approached the subject with Deputies Chen and Vega. Deputy Vega kicked the weapon away from the subject's reach. Upon inspection, Deputy Vega determined the weapon appeared to be a replica or BB gun. Deputies assessed the subject's condition and began administering first aid.

At approximately 0125 hours, OCFA Engine #19 arrived on scene. Paramedics initiated treatment. At 0134 hours, the subject was transported by ambulance to Mission Hospital.

At approximately 0131 hours, I made contact with a resident at 22851 Loumont Drive. The resident stated that no one in the household had called 911, and they had been asleep when the commotion began.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (four deputies)
  - Eight (8) cartridge cases
  - One (1) black handgun (determined to be a BB gun)
  - Cell phone belonging to subject

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to Mission Hospital. Subject subsequently pronounced deceased. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
