INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-035
Date of Incident: September 26, 2019
Time of Incident: Approximately 0245–0315 hours
Location: 1250 East La Habra Boulevard, La Habra, CA (Metro Auto Sales / Express Auto Recon)
Reporting Officer: Officer A. Kowalski
Badge Number: 5127

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Marcus Delgado, Male, 29 years old, Hispanic, visible tattoos
  Officer(s): Officer A. Kowalski, K9 Officer R. Falk, Trainee C. Vega, Officer J. Navarro, Sergeant B. Ingram, Sergeant M. Fuentes
  Witness(es): Auto body shop owner (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On September 26, 2019, at approximately 0245 hours, dispatch received a 911 call from a named witness reporting subjects breaking into vehicles near Alpine Street and La Habra Boulevard. At approximately 0249 hours, I indicated I would respond along with K9 Officer Falk and Trainee Vega.

Upon arrival, I observed a black Cadillac Escalade parked inside the fenced lot at the rear of Metro Auto Sales. The vehicle's engine was off and no lights were visible. I exited my patrol vehicle and approached the passenger side of the Escalade. I immediately observed the subject in the driver's seat and a second individual in the rear passenger seat. I issued verbal commands for both occupants to exit the vehicle and show their hands. The subject responded clearly in English that he understood and would comply when he was ready. Body-worn cameras were activated from the outset of the contact.

K9 Officer Falk arrived approximately fifteen minutes after my initial contact with the subject. He positioned the K9 vehicle at the west edge of the property and began issuing PA commands from the driver's side. The subject immediately became hostile, producing a black handgun from under his seat and waving it toward officers through the driver's side window. Officers took cover and called for additional units.

Over the next approximately five minutes, the subject intermittently revved the engine and yelled. At approximately 0258 hours, Sergeant Fuentes arrived and authorized deployment of less-lethal munitions. A single 40mm OC round was deployed through the front windshield, which immediately caused the subject to cover his face and cease yelling.

Within approximately thirty seconds, the subject placed the vehicle in drive and accelerated straight forward through the gate at what I estimated to be approximately 45 miles per hour. The vehicle struck one parked car in the lot before continuing through the metal rollup door on the far side. The entire sequence from initial acceleration to exit through the building took less than ten seconds.

Upon emerging on the South Bedford Street side, the Escalade came to a stop on the apron of the driveway. I had repositioned to the west sidewalk approximately thirty feet from the Escalade. Sergeant Ingram was positioned approximately forty feet to the south on South Bedford Street. The subject raised his right arm holding the previously observed handgun and pointed it directly at Sergeant Ingram. I discharged my patrol rifle four rounds in approximately four seconds. Sergeant Ingram discharged his sidearm five rounds. Officer Navarro also discharged her sidearm three rounds from a position near the gate. The vehicle stopped after the shots were fired.

Paramedics from Los Angeles County Fire Department arrived within two minutes of the shooting and transported the subject to a local hospital, where he was pronounced deceased approximately one hour later. A loaded semi-automatic pistol was recovered from the subject's lap during the vehicle search.
