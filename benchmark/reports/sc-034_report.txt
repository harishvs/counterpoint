INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-034
Date of Incident: June 24, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately 0115 hours
Location: 22800 block of Loumont Drive, Lake Forest, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy K. Padilla
Badge Number: 6204

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Female, approximately 25-30 years old, dressed in dark clothing with hooded sweatshirt
  Officer(s): Deputy K. Padilla, Deputy L. Orozco, Deputy T. Yoon, Deputy J. Faulkner
  Witness(es): None at scene

NARRATIVE:

On June 24, 2020, at approximately 0115 hours, Orange County Communications Center received a 911 call from a cell phone reporting a residential burglary in progress at 22851 Loumont Drive, Lake Forest. The caller reported hearing glass breaking and stated that an intruder had entered the home through a front window. Dispatch noted the caller was a female and that she and her boyfriend were sheltering inside the residence.

Deputy Orozco was initially dispatched to the location. Deputies Padilla, Yoon, and Faulkner also responded due to the nature of the call. While responding, dispatch informed us that the caller's line had gone dead and could not be reestablished.

I was the first unit to arrive at approximately 0105 hours. I parked four houses west on Loumont Drive and proceeded on foot using my flashlight. Within approximately thirty seconds of beginning my approach, I observed the subject standing under a tree on the sidewalk south of the residence, approximately 25 to 30 feet from my position. The subject was wearing all black with the hood of her sweatshirt pulled over her head. I illuminated the subject with my flashlight and observed that she was holding what appeared to be a black handgun at her side in her right hand, pointed toward the ground.

I drew my service weapon and took a position of cover behind a gray sedan parked on the street. I immediately began giving verbal commands, ordering the subject to show her hands, drop the weapon, and get on the ground. The subject did not comply and appeared frozen in place.

Deputies Orozco and Yoon arrived approximately five minutes after my initial contact with the subject and took cover positions behind their vehicles. The three of us continued issuing verbal commands over the following several minutes. The subject slowly began walking in my direction, holding the weapon at shoulder height with both hands. She then reversed course and walked back toward the tree.

Deputy Faulkner arrived last and retrieved a rifle from his vehicle before joining our position. At that point the subject had been standing under the tree for approximately ten minutes since my initial contact. The subject then abruptly turned and pointed the handgun in the direction of the deputies. I discharged my firearm three times. Deputy Yoon discharged his firearm five times. The subject immediately collapsed and the weapon dropped from her hands.

We approached the subject and secured the weapon. Deputies began administering first aid. Paramedics arrived at approximately 0125 hours, approximately ten minutes after the shooting. The subject was transported by ambulance. She was later pronounced deceased at the hospital.

Upon examination, the weapon was determined to be a Daisy 340 BB Spring Repeater Pistol, a realistic replica. Investigation subsequently revealed the 911 call had been placed from the subject's own phone, and no burglary had occurred at the reported address.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Patrol vehicle video recordings from all responding units
  - Ten (10) cartridge casings recovered from the scene
  - One (1) Daisy 340 BB Spring Repeater Pistol, black
  - Subject's clothing items
  - Subject's cell phone and California Driver's License

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital, later pronounced deceased. Scene secured for OCDASAU investigation.
