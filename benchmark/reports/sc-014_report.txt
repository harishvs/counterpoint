INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-014
Date of Incident: August 6, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1908 hours
Location: Front entrance, Ridgemont Police Department, 150 North Euclid Street, La Habra, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer L. Castillo
Badge Number: 3592

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Daniel Kwon, Male, 22 years old, Asian, black T-shirt, black pants, black Converse shoes
  Officer(s): Officer L. Castillo, Officer R. Webb
  Witness(es): Female victim/complainant (W-1, reporting party for tailgating complaint)

NARRATIVE:

On August 6, 2021, at approximately 1905 hours, I was dispatched to the front of the Ridgemont Police Department in response to a report of an aggressive driver who had followed a female motorist (W-1) from the freeway to the station. Officer Webb was dispatched simultaneously. Each of us drove a separate marked black and white police vehicle and wore department-issued navy-blue uniforms with badges and body-worn cameras.

Officer Webb and I arrived at approximately 1908 hours. I parked behind a dark blue 1998 Toyota Camry that was parked behind W-1's vehicle on the pedestrian sidewalk in front of the station. Officer Webb parked his unit behind mine. The station lobby was open and staffed at the time of our arrival.

I approached W-1, who remained seated in her vehicle. W-1 stated that a male driving the Toyota had been following her since she exited the northbound 57 freeway at Lambert Road. W-1 stated she recognized the individual as someone she had seen in her neighborhood before. W-1 appeared frightened and was still on the phone with dispatch.

Meanwhile, Officer Webb approached the subject, later identified as Daniel Kwon, who was standing at the front entrance of the station near the intercom. Officer Webb asked the subject what was going on. The subject stated he was "just waiting for a friend." Officer Webb asked the subject whether he was involved with the vehicles parked in front of the station, and the subject said he was not. Officer Webb remained facing the subject and continued to question him.

Without warning, the subject produced a firearm from his right jacket pocket, racked the slide, and pointed it at Officer Webb from a distance of approximately 15 feet. I saw the subject raise the weapon and immediately shouted, "Drop the weapon! Drop it now!" The subject then fired three rounds at Officer Webb. One round struck Officer Webb's ballistic vest in the left upper chest area, causing him to stumble backward but remain standing.

I drew my department-issued firearm and fired three rounds at the subject. One round struck the subject in the torso, causing him to fall to the ground. After the subject fell, I maintained my position and continued to issue commands for the subject to drop the firearm and show his hands.

At approximately 1910 hours, I radioed that a suspect was down and that Officer Webb had been struck. Additional officers arrived at approximately 1911 hours to render aid to Officer Webb. Officers formulated a plan to approach the subject, and at approximately 1916 hours, the subject was disarmed, moved to the sidewalk, and handcuffed.

At approximately 1920 hours, paramedics from the Los Angeles County Fire Department arrived and determined the subject had no pulse or respiration. The subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 1926 hours.

A subsequent search recovered two firearms from the subject's person: a Glock-style 9mm semi-automatic pistol with no serial number and a .40 caliber Glock semi-automatic pistol with a mounted flashlight.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
