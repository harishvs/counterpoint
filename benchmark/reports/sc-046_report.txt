PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Officer N. Blackwell, Officer S. Park

NARRATIVE:

On October 10, 2021, at approximately 2100 hours, officers responded to reports of a subject in a vehicle who was behaving erratically and may have been armed. Officer Park and I arrived on scene in marked patrol vehicles.

We observed the subject seated in the driver's seat of a vehicle. As we approached, the subject started the engine. Officers gave verbal commands in both English and Spanish for the subject to turn off the vehicle and show his hands.

The subject did not comply and produced a firearm. The subject fired two rounds toward officers from inside the vehicle. Officer Park and I returned fire. I fired multiple rounds from my duty rifle. The subject was struck and the vehicle came to a stop.

Officers approached the vehicle and found two firearms: a handgun and a rifle. Officers rendered the scene safe and provided medical aid. The subject was pronounced deceased.

  - Body-worn camera footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
