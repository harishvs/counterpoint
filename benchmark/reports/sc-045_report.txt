INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-045
Date of Incident: September 23, 2020
Time of Incident: Approximately Approximately 1324 hours
Location: 100 block of Avenida San Pablo, San Clemente, CA
Reporting Officer: Deputy F. Harmon
Badge Number: 2876

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Deputy F. Harmon, Deputy J. Calloway

NARRATIVE:

On September 23, 2020, at approximately 1324 hours, Deputy Calloway and I observed a pedestrian jaywalking across a roadway in the 100 block of Avenida San Pablo, San Clemente. We were in plain clothes driving an unmarked vehicle. Deputy Calloway was driving. We contacted the subject to discuss the infraction.

The subject initially refused to provide identification and stated, "I didn't jaywalk." The encounter escalated when the subject attempted to walk away. Officers followed on foot, attempting de-escalation. The subject stated he did not want to be detained.

A physical altercation ensued during which the subject attempted to grab Deputy Calloway's firearm from the holster. Deputy Calloway yelled for the subject to let go. A bystander was heard calling out "Shoot him again!" during the incident.

Deputy Calloway discharged his firearm during the struggle. Officers immediately began administering CPR. Paramedics were summoned to the scene.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
