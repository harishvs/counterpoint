INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-036
Date of Incident: January 8, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 0837 hours
Location: Warner Avenue near Compass Avenue, Tustin, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer P. Kessler
Badge Number: 6104

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Arturo Medina, Male, 52 years old, Hispanic, grey jacket, dark pants
  Officer(s): Officer P. Kessler (handling officer), Officer R. Navarro (backup), Officer L. Huang (less-lethal)
  Witness(es): Multiple reporting parties, Santa Ana PD Officers who had prior contact

NARRATIVE:

On Sunday, January 8, 2023, at approximately 0837 hours, Tustin Police Department dispatch received a call from a citizen reporting a male Hispanic walking eastbound on Warner Avenue near Red Hill Avenue carrying a large chef's knife. The caller stated the subject appeared to have blood on his clothing. I was designated as the handling officer and immediately responded. Officer Navarro was dispatched as backup approximately five minutes later.

Prior to our contact, Santa Ana Police Department officers had encountered the subject approximately two hours earlier near St. Andrew Place. SAPD officers reported that they had approached the subject on foot, conducted a brief pat-down search, and determined no crime had been committed. The subject was released without incident.

Upon arrival at approximately 0847 hours, Officer Navarro and I both exited our patrol vehicles simultaneously and drew our service weapons. We observed the subject walking southbound on the east sidewalk of Warner Avenue carrying a knife in his left hand. We immediately moved into the northbound lanes of Warner Avenue to establish a contact position. I began issuing verbal commands, directing the subject to drop the knife and take a seat.

Over the next several minutes, I issued approximately twenty verbal commands to the subject, including variations of "Drop the knife," "Have a seat," and "Don't come towards us." Officer Huang arrived during this extended verbal exchange and took a less-lethal position with his Taser. The subject gradually slowed his pace and appeared to be considering compliance.

Without warning, the subject suddenly charged toward Officer Navarro from approximately 32 feet away. Officer Huang deployed his Taser, which struck the subject but failed to achieve neuromuscular incapacitation. Immediately after the Taser deployment, Officer Navarro discharged his firearm three times and I discharged my firearm seven times. The subject fell to the ground in the number one lane of northbound Warner Avenue. The knife fell from his hand upon impact with the pavement.

Officer Navarro immediately secured the knife while I began providing first aid to the subject. I initiated CPR within approximately thirty seconds of the shooting. Orange County Fire Authority paramedics arrived within three minutes and assumed patient care. Despite extensive life-saving efforts, the subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 0916 hours.

A subsequent investigation revealed that the subject had no prior contacts with law enforcement in the Tustin area. A welfare check at his residence found no additional victims or evidence of prior violence.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (Officers Kessler and Navarro; Officer Huang's PVS delayed activation)
  - Seven (7) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - One (1) large chef's knife with black handle
  - Clothing items from the subject

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased on scene. Scene secured for OCDA investigation. Welfare check conducted at subject's residence with negative results.
