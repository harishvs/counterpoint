INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-026
Date of Incident: March 3-4, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0200-0415 hours
Location: 615 South Resh Street, Anaheim, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer K. Brandt (Tactical Negotiations Unit)
Badge Number: 6104

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Terrance Mitchell, Male, 41 years old, Black
  Officer(s): Officer K. Brandt (negotiator), Officer J. Franco, Officer D. Pak, Officer A. Linden, Officer R. Cortez (armored vehicle), Officer M. Hassan (less lethal), Officer T. Nolan (K9)
  Witness(es): Subject's ex-girlfriend (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On the evening of March 3, 2022, Anaheim Police Department received a call for service from W-1, the subject's ex-girlfriend, at approximately 2144 hours. W-1 reported that the subject had pointed a handgun at her inside the residence at 615 South Resh Street, threatened to kill her and the neighbors, and fired a shot inside the home. W-1 fled the residence and met with responding officers. She stated that the subject owned two firearms and had not been taking his prescribed psychiatric medication.

Patrol units established a perimeter around the residence. Communication attempts via phone were unsuccessful. At approximately 0005 hours on March 4, the Tactical Response Group was activated. Upon arrival, SWAT officers immediately assumed control of the perimeter and positioned an armored vehicle in front of the residence. I was designated as the primary negotiator and began PA announcements at approximately 0210 hours, urging the subject to exit peacefully.

At approximately 0236 hours, the subject first appeared at the front door. He stepped onto the front porch, yelled at officers, and returned inside after approximately 45 seconds. He repeated this behavior at 0244 hours and 0251 hours, each time briefly yelling before retreating inside. During the third appearance at 0251 hours, I observed the subject make a finger-gun gesture toward the armored vehicle, which I documented in my notes.

At approximately 0310 hours, in response to a recorded surrender request from W-1, the subject exited the front door holding what appeared to be an AR-15 style rifle. The subject held the weapon at his side and stood on the porch for approximately 15 seconds before returning inside without pointing the weapon at officers. Immediately after this appearance, at approximately 0312 hours, the subject emerged again holding a small revolver in his left hand. The subject raised the revolver, pointed it toward the sky, and then lowered it toward the armored vehicle where Officers Franco and Pak were positioned.

Officers Franco, Pak, Linden, and Cortez discharged their rifles. The subject fell to the ground. An arrest team approached and found the subject seated against the front wall of the residence with the revolver on the ground beside him. Officer Hassan deployed a 40 mm Less Lethal round as a precaution. When the subject showed no response, Officer Nolan deployed the K9 to secure the subject. The K9 pulled the subject from the porch onto the front lawn. Officers then began administering CPR.

Anaheim Fire Department paramedics, who had been staged in the area, were summoned and arrived at approximately 0330 hours. Paramedics applied a cardiac monitor and determined the subject was in asystole. The subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 0342 hours.

A search of the residence and porch area recovered one firearm: the .38 caliber Smith and Wesson revolver found beside the subject on the porch. No other firearms were located.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from multiple officers
  - Audio dispatch and radio traffic recordings
  - Ring video doorbell recording
  - One (1) Smith & Wesson .38 caliber revolver
  - Rifle cartridge cases collected from scene

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject pronounced deceased at scene. Residence searched and secured. W-1 interviewed at Command Post.
