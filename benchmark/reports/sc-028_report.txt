INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-028
Date of Incident: December 24, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0045 hours
Location: 9200 block of Bolsa Avenue, Westminster, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer C. Ngo
Badge Number: 4102

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Duc Phan, Male, 30 years old, Asian, gray jacket, blue jeans
  Officer(s): Officer C. Ngo, Officer L. Trang, Officer P. DeSilva
  Witness(es): Store Owner (reporting party)

NARRATIVE:

On December 24, 2022, at approximately 0045 hours, I was dispatched to a business at 9196 Bolsa Avenue in the City of Westminster regarding an incident of vandalism. I was wearing my WPD long-sleeved utility uniform with identification patches and a black tactical vest marked "POLICE." I had a Body Worn Camera mounted on my vest. I arrived on scene at approximately 0055 hours, approximately ten minutes after receiving the dispatch.

Upon arrival, the store owner informed me that a male subject had struck the front of his business and an exterior surveillance camera with a metal object. The store owner showed me cell phone footage from his surveillance system depicting the incident. He stated the subject had left the area roughly twenty minutes before my arrival.

At approximately 0056 hours, I located the subject, later identified as Duc Phan, in the south alley behind 9262 Bolsa Avenue. He was wearing a gray jacket and blue jeans and carrying a metal pole approximately 30 inches long in his right hand. I exited my vehicle and ordered the subject to stop, drop the weapon, and sit down. The subject refused all commands and walked westbound away from me.

I drew my service weapon and followed the subject on foot westbound through the alley toward Bolsa Avenue. Approximately ninety seconds later, Officer Trang arrived at the scene and joined me on foot. Together we continued following the subject as he turned northbound onto the sidewalk along Bolsa Avenue. Officer Trang and I gave commands in English only throughout the encounter. The subject remained non-compliant but walked at a slow, steady pace without any aggressive gestures.

The foot pursuit continued westbound on Bolsa Avenue for approximately two blocks over a period of roughly eight minutes. During this time the subject made no threatening movements and kept the pole at his side. At approximately 0103 hours, Officer DeSilva arrived and the three of us continued following the subject into a shopping center parking lot at 9081 Bolsa Avenue.

Once in the parking lot, the subject suddenly stopped and turned toward officers. He held the pole loosely at waist level. I again commanded the subject to drop the weapon. After a brief pause of approximately fifteen seconds, the subject raised the pole to roughly chest height. Believing the subject was preparing to strike, I discharged my firearm two times. Officer Trang simultaneously deployed his TASER. The subject dropped the pole and fell to the ground.

Officer DeSilva immediately moved in and handcuffed the subject, who was conscious but did not speak. I holstered my weapon and began administering first aid. We requested paramedics, who arrived within approximately three minutes and transported the subject to a local hospital. The subject survived his injuries, which I understand were limited to a wound to his right arm.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage (Officers Ngo, Trang, DeSilva)
  - Two (2) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - One (1) Staccato C2, 9mm semi-automatic handgun
  - One (1) TASER unit
  - One (1) metal pole, approximately 30 inches
  - Surveillance camera footage from store owner

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to local hospital, survived. Scene secured for investigation.
