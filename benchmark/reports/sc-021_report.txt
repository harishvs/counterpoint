NARRATIVE:

On March 10, 2026, at approximately 1515 hours, I was dispatched to a residence on Maplewood Drive in Pleasant Grove, UT, in response to a 911 call. The caller, later identified as the homeowner, reported that an unknown male was standing near his front door and had begun knocking aggressively on the residence. The caller stated he was afraid for the safety of his family.

Upon arrival, I observed a dark blue sedan parked directly in front of the victim's residence on the street. I approached the vehicle and observed a male in the driver's seat, later identified as Nathan Parker. A female, later identified as Megan Holt, was seated in the front passenger seat. I asked Parker if he had been knocking on the residence door. Parker stated, "Yeah, we need to talk to him about what he owes us."

I asked Parker what they were doing at the residence. Parker stated he was a licensed private investigator working on behalf of a client involved in a civil dispute over a retail franchise. Parker said the homeowner had stolen approximately $500,000 worth of merchandise from his client and that he was here to personally serve legal documents. Parker identified Holt as his legal assistant.

I asked Parker who his client was. Parker replied that his client preferred to remain anonymous and that the matter was confidential. Holt did not speak during the initial contact and appeared to be recording the interaction on her cell phone.

I then made contact with the homeowner inside the residence. The homeowner stated that Parker and his associates had been coming to the property every day for the past week, knocking on his door, photographing his house, and placing signs on public property near his home. The homeowner stated he had contacted police on two prior occasions regarding similar conduct. The homeowner described being afraid for the safety of himself, his wife, and his children.

While officers were speaking with all parties, I observed a sign that had been placed on the sidewalk directly in front of the victim's residence. The sign displayed a photograph of the homeowner standing next to stacks of cash with the text "I ROBBED A FAMILY BUSINESS." I asked Parker if he had placed the sign. Parker denied placing it and stated it must have been left by someone else.

After reviewing the circumstances of this incident and the two prior incidents, I determined there was probable cause to arrest Parker for stalking and targeted residential picketing. I placed Parker in handcuffs and conducted a search of his person. During the search, I located a folding knife in Parker's front right pants pocket. Parker was transported by Officer Nguyen to the Pleasant Grove Police Department and subsequently by Officer Morales to the county jail.

I performed a criminal history check on Parker and found two prior misdemeanor convictions for trespassing and harassment.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
