INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-052
Date of Incident: February 19, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately 1015 hours
Location: Parking lot, La Quinta Inn, 1515 South Coast Drive, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Navarro
Badge Number: 5831

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Carlos Fuentes, Male, 34 years old, Hispanic
  Officer(s): Officer D. Navarro, Officer R. Pham
  Witness(es): Female front passenger (W-1), Male left rear passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On February 19, 2021, at approximately 1015 hours, Officer Pham and I were assigned to the Community Policing Unit and were patrolling in a marked CMPD police vehicle. Officer Pham was driving the patrol vehicle and I was seated in the front passenger seat.

While patrolling the parking lot of the La Quinta Inn at 1515 South Coast Drive, I located an occupied gray 2006 Ford Focus parked in a marked parking stall facing south. I observed the subject seated in the driver seat with his head slumped forward on the steering wheel. Based on my training and experience, I believed the subject may have been under the influence of narcotics.

I approached the driver side of the vehicle while Officer Pham approached the passenger side. I observed three additional occupants: W-1 in the front passenger seat and W-2 in the left rear seat. I recognized W-2 from prior narcotics investigation contacts. Officer Pham searched W-2 and located drug paraphernalia on his person. W-2 was placed in handcuffs with his hands behind his back.

I conversed with the subject. I observed the subject's right hand was bandaged. I instructed the subject to keep his hands on the steering wheel. However, the subject repeatedly moved his left hand toward the center console, his waistband, and the area of his left leg. I was concerned the subject may have had a weapon and he had not been searched.

Officer Pham had W-1 step out of the vehicle and move behind a vehicle parked east of the Ford Focus where he searched her. W-2 was seated on the asphalt behind the right rear bumper of the Ford Focus.

Without warning, the subject suddenly started the vehicle, placed it in reverse, and accelerated. Officer Pham and I shouted commands for the subject to stop. Officer Pham was unsuccessful in pulling W-2 from the path of the Ford Focus as it reversed. The right rear tire ran over W-2's handcuffed hands. W-2 was then struck by the open passenger door and front left tire.

Both Officer Pham and I fired our department-issued handguns at the subject. I fired three times and Officer Pham fired twice. The left rear bumper of the Ford Focus struck the right front bumper of our parked police vehicle. The subject then put the Ford Focus in drive and started moving forward. Officer Pham fired three additional times through the windshield. I fired once toward the subject from the driver side.

Officers provided CPR until Costa Mesa Fire Department paramedics arrived. The subject was transported by ambulance to Orange County Global Medical Center where the subject was later pronounced deceased. W-2 was also transported to OCGMC and treated for non-life-threatening injuries.

A Hellcat .380 semi-automatic handgun was located in the right pocket of the subject's jacket. The handgun contained a loaded magazine with one round in the chamber.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased. W-2 treated for non-life-threatening injuries. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
