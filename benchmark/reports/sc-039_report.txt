INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-039
Date of Incident: August 30, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 2136 hours
Location: Old Ranch Condominium, 3600 block of Aster Street, Seal Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer K. Thornton
Badge Number: 7234

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Officer K. Thornton, Corporal V. Estrada

NARRATIVE:

On August 30, 2023, at approximately 2136 hours, Corporal Estrada and I were dispatched to a residence in the Old Ranch Condominium development regarding a male subject attempting to kick down the caller's front door. The caller described the male as wearing a jacket, mask, beanie, and dark brown work boots.

I arrived at approximately 2140 hours and observed a male subject, later identified as the subject, peering over the brick perimeter wall of the enclosed rear patio area. Only his head was visible. I stood in the walkway approximately 40 feet east and used my flashlight to illuminate the subject. I identified myself and commanded him to come out of the enclosed area. The subject stated he was there to see the reporting party. I informed him the reporting party had called police and did not want him there.

I instructed the subject to show me his hands. He refused and told me to stop shining the flashlight on his face. At approximately 2142 hours, Corporal Estrada arrived and parked approximately 200 to 300 feet south. The subject then disappeared behind the patio wall. I drew my duty weapon. At approximately 2142 hours, the subject exited the patio area wearing a baggy black jacket, blue jeans, a black hat, and clear glasses. He had a beverage can in his right hand.

I ordered the subject to put his hands up. He ignored my commands and continued walking toward me. He moved the beverage can from his right hand to his left hand. He placed his right hand into his right front jacket pocket and turned back toward the enclosed patio. Corporal Estrada warned he would deploy a Taser. At approximately 2142 hours, Corporal Estrada deployed his Taser, striking the subject in the back. The subject fell onto his left side and then flat on his back.

The subject reached toward his right-side waistband and drew what appeared to be a black Glock handgun. He pointed the weapon in the direction of Corporal Estrada. I fired my duty weapon 21 rounds. Corporal Estrada fired nine rounds from his handgun. The subject stated "Finish me off" while on his back. Officers held him at gunpoint until a third officer arrived at approximately 2148 hours, removed the gun from near the subject's right hand, and handcuffed him. OCFA Engine 84 arrived at approximately 2152 hours. The weapon was a Glock 22, .40-caliber, registered to the subject's father.

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
