INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-044
Date of Incident: April 28, 2021
Time of Incident: Approximately Approximately 0430 hours
Location: Long Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer C. Morrow
Badge Number: 9456

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Officer(s): Officer C. Morrow, Multiple units

NARRATIVE:

On April 28, 2021, at approximately 0430 hours, officers initiated a pursuit of a stolen vehicle being driven by the subject at speeds exceeding 90 miles per hour on the wrong side of the road. The pursuit lasted approximately seven minutes, during which multiple patrol units were involved.

A PIT maneuver was executed in the southbound lanes, causing the stolen vehicle to collide with multiple parked cars. Three of the four tires fell off the rims as a result of the collision. The vehicle came to a stop and three patrol units positioned themselves approximately 15 feet away.

The subject remained seated in the driver's seat for less than 15 seconds. He then exited the vehicle, yelled profanities, and made threatening gestures toward officers with both hands moving to his right hip area. Officers perceived a threat and one officer fired six rounds from his duty weapon. Another officer deployed a TASER. The subject fell to the ground.

Officers broadcast shots fired and TASER deployment over the radio. Paramedics were requested. The subject was later determined to be unarmed.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Scene secured for investigation.
