INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-025
Date of Incident: July 13, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 0520 hours
Location: Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Tran
Badge Number: 2784

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Marco Delgado, Male, 35 years old, Hispanic
  Officer(s): Officer D. Tran, Officer C. Webb, Sgt. M. Park, Officer K. Ruiz, Officer A. Mateo
  Witness(es): Area resident (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On July 13, 2022, at approximately 0520 hours, Officer C. Webb and I responded to an ADT commercial burglary alarm activation at Advanced Marine Service, 2211 Newport Boulevard, Costa Mesa. We arrived on scene within approximately two minutes of the dispatch call and immediately proceeded to the front of the building. Both officers were in department-issued uniforms and marked vehicles equipped with body-worn cameras, which were activated upon arrival.

I walked the exterior perimeter of the building alone while Officer Webb remained at the front entrance to secure it. During my perimeter check, I contacted an area resident (W-1) who stated she had seen an individual using a hammer to damage property inside the business. I then located a broken front door window with glass on the ground. I scaled a locked security gate and took up an observation position approximately 15 to 20 feet from the front door, from which I could see the subject inside the business going through cabinets while holding a knife.

I radioed for additional units. Within approximately ten minutes, Sgt. Park, Officer Ruiz, and Officer Mateo arrived on scene. At approximately 0528 hours, Sgt. Park and Officer Ruiz approached the front doors. They identified themselves and ordered the subject to drop the knife and exit the building. The subject briefly looked in their direction, then turned back and continued pacing through the business. He appeared calm and did not exhibit threatening behavior during this period.

At approximately 0530 hours, Sgt. Park developed a tactical plan. Officer Mateo was designated to deploy a Taser as the less-lethal option. Sgt. Park obtained a 40mm less-lethal launcher and positioned himself near the security gate. Over the next several minutes, the subject wandered through different rooms. At approximately 0533 hours, the subject emerged from a room on the southwest side of the building carrying a rifle in his right hand.

Officers immediately ordered the subject to drop the weapon. The subject hesitated briefly near the interior doorway, then walked toward the front of the building. As the subject raised the rifle toward the broken window, I discharged my firearm approximately four times. Officers Ruiz and Park also fired. In total, officers fired approximately fifteen rounds. The subject collapsed inside the business.

Officers immediately approached the subject, who had fallen approximately three feet from the front window. We forced entry within approximately thirty seconds of the last shot and began rendering medical aid. Costa Mesa Fire Department paramedics arrived within five minutes and the subject was transported to the hospital. The subject was barely conscious and unresponsive during transport.

The rifle recovered from the scene was later identified as belonging to the subject. It was a semi-automatic weapon chambered in .22 caliber with a full magazine.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from all officers on scene
  - Fifteen (15) cartridge cases
  - One (1) rifle recovered from inside the business
  - Knife
  - Multiple bullet fragments
  - Glass from broken windows

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to hospital. Scene secured for investigation. Business owner notified.
