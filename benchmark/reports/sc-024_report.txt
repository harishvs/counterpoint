INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-024
Date of Incident: January 16, 2023
Time of Incident: Approximately 2136 hours
Location: Old Ranch Condominium development, Seal Beach, CA
Reporting Officer: Corporal M. Cervantes
Badge Number: 2764

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Nathan Collier, Male, 47 years old, White, dark jacket, jeans, hat, clear-lens glasses
  Officer(s): Corporal M. Cervantes, Officer A. Pruitt
  Witness(es): Female resident (W-1), two minor children present in residence

NARRATIVE:

On January 16, 2023, at approximately 2136 hours, SBPD dispatch received a 9-1-1 call from a female resident (W-1) reporting that an unknown male was attempting to force entry into her residence at the Old Ranch Condominium development. W-1 stated she was home alone with her two young children. She described the male as wearing a jacket, beanie, and dark boots. The residence is subject to an active domestic violence restraining order prohibiting a former partner from approaching within 50 yards.

I was dispatched to the location and arrived at approximately 2140 hours, ahead of Officer Pruitt. The condominium has a rear courtyard enclosed by an approximately eight-foot brick wall with a gate. I observed the subject standing at the courtyard gate at approximately 2141 hours. I stood approximately 40 feet east of his position and illuminated the subject with my flashlight. I identified myself as SBPD and commanded the subject to step away from the residence.

The subject responded that he was visiting a friend and that she had invited him over. I continued issuing commands. The subject appeared agitated and was pacing. Officer Pruitt arrived on scene approximately four minutes after I made initial contact with the subject. Officer Pruitt parked his vehicle and proceeded on foot to my position.

The subject became increasingly noncompliant. He concealed his left hand inside his jacket, turned, and began walking back toward the patio entrance. I gave loud verbal commands for the subject to stop and remove his hands from his clothing. The subject paused momentarily and faced me, then turned and entered the enclosed patio area. At that point, I warned the subject I would deploy my Taser. When the subject continued walking away, I deployed my Taser, striking the subject in the upper back. The subject fell forward onto his stomach on the patio floor.

Immediately after the Taser deployment, Officer Pruitt advanced to the patio threshold. The subject rolled from his stomach to his back, reached toward his left hip, and produced a black handgun. The subject pointed the weapon upward in my general direction. Officer Pruitt, fearing for my safety, discharged his firearm, firing seven rounds. I simultaneously transitioned from my Taser to my duty weapon and discharged nine rounds.

After confirming the threat was neutralized, we held our positions and maintained cover. During this time, the subject was heard to say, "Just let me die." The subject's movements near the weapon continued intermittently for approximately three minutes before additional units arrived. Officer Holt arrived at approximately 2143 hours and secured the handgun. The weapon was a Glock 22, .40-caliber, loaded with one in the chamber and five in the magazine.

OCFA Engine 84 arrived at approximately 2148 hours and transported the subject to UCI Medical Center. The subject was pronounced deceased at approximately 2232 hours. A subsequent search found no additional weapons on the subject's person.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from Corporal Cervantes and Officer Pruitt
  - One (1) Glock 22, .40-caliber handgun with six (6) live rounds
  - Sixteen (16) cartridge cases, WIN 9MM LUGER
  - Clothing and personal effects

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to UCI Medical Center, pronounced deceased. W-1 and children confirmed uninjured. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
