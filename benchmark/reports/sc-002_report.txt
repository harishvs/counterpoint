INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-002
Date of Incident: November 16, 2022
Time of Incident: Approximately 1400 hours
Location: 100 S. McClay Street, Santa Ana, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer R. Navarro
Badge Number: 5831

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Diego Renteria, Male, 41 years old, Hispanic, grey jacket, beige shorts
  Officer(s): Officer R. Navarro, Officer K. Cho
  Witness(es): Male driver (W-1), Male rear-seat passenger (W-2)

NARRATIVE:

On November 16, 2022, at approximately 1400 hours, Officer Cho and I were conducting Directed Enforcement Patrol in a marked black and white SAPD patrol unit in the area of First Avenue and McClay Street, Santa Ana. I was operating the patrol vehicle and Officer Cho was seated in the right front passenger seat. Both officers were wearing SAPD polo uniform shirts with identification patches and black outer vests clearly marked "POLICE." Both officers had body-worn cameras activated and recording.

At approximately 1400 hours, I initiated a traffic stop on a black Mercedes Benz, California license plate 8YQZ992, at 100 S. McClay Street, after observing that the vehicle's registration tags had expired. I positioned the patrol unit approximately five feet behind the Mercedes. The driver of the Mercedes was subsequently identified as W-1, who was alone in the vehicle with the subject, Diego Renteria, in the front passenger seat.

Upon contacting W-1, I advised him the stop was for expired registration. W-1 stated he was on his way to the DMV to renew the registration. I asked all occupants if they were on probation or parole, and W-1 responded that he was on AB109 supervision. Based on this information, I asked W-1 to step out, handcuffed him for officer safety, conducted a pat-down search, and placed him in the rear of the patrol car.

I then approached the right rear passenger side of the Mercedes and contacted the subject, Renteria. Officer Cho was positioned on the driver's side of the Mercedes near the front fender. The subject had a bag of fast food in his hands which he placed on the center console. The subject then picked up his cell phone and began texting. I asked the subject to put down his phone and place his hands on the dashboard. The subject complied and placed both hands flat on the dashboard.

After a short pause, the subject's right hand dropped off the dashboard and he began reaching toward his right side near the floorboard area. I repeated my command for the subject to put his hands on the dash. The subject then abruptly reached down to his right side with his right hand and produced a black semi-automatic handgun. I observed the subject grip the weapon and begin to raise it in the direction of Officer Cho, who was standing on the driver's side.

I immediately drew my service weapon and discharged it four times at the subject through the open rear passenger window. Simultaneously, Officer Cho discharged his firearm three times from his position near the driver's side front fender. After both officers ceased fire, we maintained our positions on the sidewalk with our weapons drawn.

I radioed dispatch to report the officer-involved shooting. Additional SAPD units arrived within approximately one minute. Officers maintained a perimeter around the Mercedes and gave repeated commands for the subject to exit the vehicle, which went unanswered. At approximately 1409 hours, a K9 unit arrived. The K9 was deployed into the vehicle at approximately 1414 hours but the subject was unresponsive. Officers then breached the vehicle and located the subject slumped in the front passenger seat. A black semi-automatic handgun was recovered from the floorboard beneath the subject's legs.

Officers removed the subject from the vehicle, handcuffed him, and began providing medical aid. OCFA paramedics arrived and transported the subject to Orange County Global Medical Center, where the subject was pronounced deceased at 1502 hours.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:
  - Body-worn camera footage from both officers (continuous recording)
  - Seven (7) cartridge cases
  - One (1) Polymer80 model PF940SC handgun, 9mm Luger caliber, no serial number
  - Two (2) high-capacity magazines (27 and 31 rounds)
  - Miscellaneous personal property including cellular phone, wallet, currency
  - Clothing items from the subject

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject transported to OCGMC, pronounced deceased at 1502 hours. W-1 released on scene. Scene secured for investigation.
