INCIDENT REPORT

Case Number: SC-050
Date of Incident: September 26, 2019
Time of Incident: Approximately 0237 hours
Location: 1250 East La Habra Boulevard (Metro Auto Sales / Express Auto Recon), La Habra, CA
Reporting Officer: Officer D. Hartman
Badge Number: 7156

PERSONS INVOLVED:
  Subject: Rafael Espinoza, Male, 29 years old, visible tattoos
  Officer(s): Officer D. Hartman, K9 Officer T. Murray, Officer Trainee C. Ramos, Sergeant E. Lowe
  Witness(es): Business owner (W-1)

NARRATIVE:

On September 26, 2019, at approximately 0237 hours, La Habra Police Department dispatch received an anonymous call reporting multiple subjects dismantling a vehicle in the area of 200 South Alpine Street. Officers Hartman, K9 Officer Murray, Officer Trainee Ramos, and Sergeant Lowe indicated they would respond.

Upon arrival, I traveled eastbound on Alpine Street and observed that the chain-link gate at the rear of Metro Auto Sales was open. I parked in the apron of the driveway and observed a black Cadillac Escalade with its lights on and engine running. I approached the vehicle on foot from the driver's side and observed the subject seated in the driver's seat.

I activated my body-worn camera, identified myself as La Habra Police, and ordered the subject to exit the vehicle. The subject initially appeared to comply, lowering the driver's side window. He briefly raised one hand and then closed the window. K9 Officer Murray arrived and deployed the K9 from the vehicle. Murray positioned himself on the east edge of the gate and began issuing K9 warnings through the PA system. I then moved to a position behind the passenger side door of the K9 vehicle.

Sergeant Lowe arrived on scene and assumed command. Lowe directed officers to deploy OC rounds to force the subject from the vehicle. Officer Ramos positioned herself on the west side of the gate. Prior to deploying OC, the subject revved the engine, turned up the radio, and began yelling obscenities in English from the sunroof. At approximately 0301 hours, Sergeant Lowe deployed the first OC round into the vehicle.

The subject then placed the vehicle in reverse and accelerated toward the K9 vehicle and officers positioned behind it. Several officers were forced to move to avoid being struck. The subject then shifted into drive, crashed through the gate, and collided with multiple parked vehicles inside the auto body shop. After crashing into the garage bay, the subject drove the Escalade through the metal rollup door and exited onto the street.

Officers then observed the Escalade emerge from the garage at high speed and accelerate eastbound on South Bedford Street. Sergeant Lowe took a position in the southbound lane. Officers Hartman and Ramos moved to the northwest corner of Alpine and Bedford. The Escalade came to rest on the apron of the driveway facing South Bedford Street.

I could see into the vehicle through the passenger window and observed the subject had a look of determination on his face. The subject raised his right arm toward the passenger-side window. Officer Murray fired seven rounds from his Colt AR-15A4 rifle. Sergeant Lowe then fired ten rounds from his Glock 21C. Officer Ramos attempted to fire but her weapon malfunctioned. The subject was struck and the Escalade came to a stop.

After securing the scene, officers discovered the business owner, W-1, who had been asleep inside the shop during the entire incident and was awakened by the collision noises.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED:

  - One (1) .40 caliber cartridge with light primer strike
  - Vehicle (Cadillac Escalade)
  - OC rounds and foam projectile

ACTIONS TAKEN:
  Subject deceased at scene. Business owner W-1 uninjured. Scene secured for OCDA investigation.
